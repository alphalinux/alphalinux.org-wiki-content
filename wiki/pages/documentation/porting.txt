===== Porting =====

Notes on the parts of the Alpha architecture that software trips over when it is first built for Alpha.

A theme runs through most of these pages: the problems are rarely Alpha problems. They are latent bugs that other architectures hide. Alpha has no byte store, no unaligned load, no dependent-load ordering and no precise arithmetic traps, so it surfaces defects that x86 silently absorbs. Where that is the case, these pages say so and cite the evidence, because the most useful thing a porting note can do is explain why a change is a genuine fix rather than an architecture-specific workaround.
