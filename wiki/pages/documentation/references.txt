===== References =====

Specifications, reference manuals, and books on the Alpha architecture and its software conventions. For Linux work, the Fourth Edition of the //Alpha Architecture Reference Manual// is the definitive description of the instruction set, memory model, and PALcode interface, and the //Digital UNIX Calling Standard for Alpha Systems// describes the calling convention Linux follows. The hardware reference manuals of the individual processors are listed under [[hardware:cpus|CPUs]], those of the core logic under [[hardware:chipsets|Chipsets]], and the firmware manuals under [[software:firmware|Firmware]].
