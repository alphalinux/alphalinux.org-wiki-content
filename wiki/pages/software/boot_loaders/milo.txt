===== MILO =====

MILO, the Alpha Linux Miniloader, is a boot loader and minimal firmware for Alpha systems. It initializes the machine, loads Linux, and provides the PALcode that Linux runs on. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 1)] It was mainly used on systems with the [[software:firmware:arc|ARC or AlphaBIOS]] firmware, whose PALcode is designed for Windows NT and cannot run Linux. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L26796|"Re: Does DEC have a clue?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Jan 1998)] Systems with the [[software:firmware:srm|SRM console]] normally boot Linux with [[software:boot_loaders:aboot|aboot]] instead.

Unlike LILO, which relies on the PC BIOS to load it, MILO is itself firmware, and it includes unmodified Linux device drivers. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 2)] David Rusling described it as "LILO with knobs on, a kind of freeware console". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-December.mbox#L21083|"Re: Confusion re SRM, ARC, MILO, etc."]], David Rusling, axp-list, 14 Dec 1996)]

The current source repository is [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo]].

==== History ====

=== Digital (1995 to 1999) ===

David A. Rusling of Digital's European Semiconductor Applications Engineering group in Reading, England, wrote MILO and its Flash Management Utility. [(>''READ.ME'', Miniloader directory, Linux-Alpha archive, ftp.digital.com, 26 Nov 1996)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L949|"Re: fmu on PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Jan 1998)] The first work was for the Noname boards, so that Linux could run on them without the license fee for the SRM console. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L32014|"Re: Linux_is_Great!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Dec 1998)] Rusling gave the same reason: "we charge for the SRM (usually the cost of a Digital Unix license) and so being able to either flash MILO or boot MILO via ARC was to bring down the costs of a Linux box." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-December.mbox#L21172|"Re: Confusion re SRM, ARC, MILO, etc."]], David Rusling, axp-list, 14 Dec 1996)] The Alpha Miniloader Howto credits Eric Rasmussen and Eilleen Samberg with the PALcode, Jim Paradis with the original MILO interface and the AlphaBIOS work, and David Mosberger-Tang with the free BIOS emulation. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 10)]

MILO first reached the public in BLADE 0.2 on 9 August 1995, described there as "the Linux/Alpha miniloader [...] a console firmware replacement which allows for the installation of Linux on systems that do not support the SRM console firmware". Digital expected it to take over: "We hope that eventually MILO will become the preferred method of loading Linux/Alpha, and that the SRM will be used only in those cases where there is no MILO support." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-os-linux-announce-mbox/googlegroups-1995-August.mbox#L4599|"BLADE 0.2 now available! (Linux for Alpha)"]], Jim Paradis, comp.os.linux.announce, 9 Aug 1995)]

MILO was distributed from ''gatekeeper.dec.com'' in ''/pub/DEC/Linux-Alpha/Miniloader''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L17436|"Re: eb164 RH 4.0"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 8 Nov 1996)] Its version numbers followed the Linux kernel it was built against. The 1.3 sources were built against Linux 1.3, 2.0 against 2.0, and 2.1 against 2.1. [(>''READ.ME'', Miniloader directory, Linux-Alpha archive, ftp.digital.com, 26 Nov 1996)] Red Hat shipped MILO images built by Rusling. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L25560|"Re: Red Hat vs Craftworks Linux AXP"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 14 Nov 1996)]

Jay Estabrook took over MILO in 1997, when Rusling moved to StrongARM work. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-July.mbox#L21443|"Re: NEW and IMPROVED Alpha Patches and MILO Sources!"]], David A Rusling, axp-list, 18 Jul 1997)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L949|"Re: fmu on PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Jan 1998)] His MILOs were placed in the ''Kernels'' directory on gatekeeper rather than ''Miniloader'', with names such as ''milo-lx164-980809''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-October.mbox#L68641|"New MILOs Available (Was: Kernel building - zImage ??)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Oct 1998)] His last source release was ''milo-2.0.35-0.4.tar.gz'', dated July 1999. [(>''Kernels'' directory, Linux-Alpha archive, ftp.digital.com)]

=== Nikita Schmidt (1998 to 2000) ===

Nikita Schmidt at University College Dublin maintained a separate branch at ''ftp://genie.ucd.ie/pub/alpha/milo''. It added ''/etc/milo.conf'', a startup command stored in the image, and the ''miloctl'' tool. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L3919|"MILO status"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 11 Jul 1998)] He described it as "Based on the latest official sources from gatekeeper. Plenty of bug fixes and new features. [...] Used by Debian and Red Hat". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L354|"Re: Milo * bootp"]], Nikita Schmidt, milo-list, 29 Jun 1999)] SuSE used it as well. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L427|"Re: Milo * bootp"]], Ruediger Oertel, milo-list, 29 Jun 1999)] Estabrook supported the effort, saying the Debian developers "seem to have a lot more time and energy and good ideas to bring to continuing MILO development than I do". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-October.mbox#L68641|"New MILOs Available (Was: Kernel building - zImage ??)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Oct 1998)] MILO 2.2 is based on his 2.0.35-c7b sources. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/README.milo|README.milo]], MILO 2.2)]

=== MILO 2.2 (1999 to 2001) ===

Stefan Reinauer of SuSE ported MILO to Linux 2.2, starting from Nikita Schmidt's sources. At first it only worked on the Ruffian. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L1|"Milo 2.2 (release)"]], Stefan Reinauer, milo-list, 9 Nov 1999)] Release 2.2-17 added initrd support and fixes for Linux 2.4. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/2000-June.mbox#L45|"[Milo-list] Milo 2.2-17"]], Stefan Reinauer, milo-list, 29 Jun 2000)] The last release, 2.2-18 in July 2001, added support for the UDB flash ROM and ReiserFS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L89|"[Milo-list] Milo 2.2-18 released."]], Stefan Reinauer, milo-list, 23 Jul 2001)] An attempt to port MILO to Linux 2.4 hung during PCI initialization on all machines. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-August.mbox#L14468|"Kernel 2.4 PCI init on CIA/PYXIS (Milo issue)"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 21 Aug 2001)] Jay Estabrook: "MILO was last ported to 2.2, not beyond." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L3285|"Re: AS2100"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 18 Jul 2001)]

In May 2002, Estabrook released test MILOs for the XLT, PC164, and Ruffian, based on 2.2-18, which could read ext2 file systems with sparse superblocks. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L492|"XLT-PC164-Ruffian MILOs available for test"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 1 May 2002)] These, with later images for the SX164 and UDB, are in the ''TESTING'' directory of the Linux-Alpha archive. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-August.mbox#L16526|"Re: Help me with RH 7.2 insatllation on 164SX :("]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Aug 2002)]

=== Decline ===

Once Windows NT for Alpha was discontinued, MILO was only needed on systems without SRM. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-June.mbox#L17559|"Re: Benefits of Linux/Alpha over Linux/x86?"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 23 Jun 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-July.mbox#L265|"Re: MILO upgrades don't work"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 2 Jul 2000)] Red Hat 7 included MILO images but did not support them. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-January.mbox#L9322|"Re: SRM?"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 8 Jan 2001)] Debian's installer for 3.1 (sarge) supported only SRM, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-January.mbox#L1757|"Calling all testers: debian-installer images for sarge/alpha"]], Steve Langasek, debian-alpha, 4 Jan 2004)] because "building MILO from source is a particularly arcane art that no one currently working on d-i has time for." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-August.mbox#L4073|"Re: AlphaServer DS25 failure"]], Steve Langasek, debian-alpha, 11 Aug 2004)] In 2004, Stefan Reinauer said he had pulled his efforts off MILO and asked for someone to continue its development. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-January.mbox#L184|"Re: Ruffian without floppy drive"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 5 Jan 2004)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-March.mbox#L758|"Ruffian without floppy drive"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 15 Mar 2004)] The Fedora 8 based Alpha CD of 2008 had no support for MILO machines. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L1|"Fedora 8-based distro for Alpha CD Announcement"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 14 Apr 2008)]

=== Current repository ===

The [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo|alphalinux-org/milo]] repository contains releases 2.2-9 to 2.2-18 as imported commits, followed by work from 2026. That work vendors the Linux 2.2.26 sources so that no external kernel tree is needed, and fixes cross-compilation for the Miata. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/commit/e5a4a2a|"vendor Linux 2.2.26 sources and fix MIATA cross-compilation"]], alphalinux-org/milo)] It also loads kernels with multiple ''PT_LOAD'' segments and skips the ''PT_NOTE'' header added in Linux 2.6.22, which had caused "Attempted to kill init!" at boot. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/commit/d27d5c8|"support kernels with multiple PT_LOAD segments"]], alphalinux-org/milo)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/commit/dd79b34|"fix(boot): skip non-PT_LOAD segments when loading kernel image"]], alphalinux-org/milo)]

==== Design ====

The Alpha Miniloader Howto lists the main parts of MILO: [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 2)]

  * PALcode,
  * memory setup, which builds page tables and turns on virtual addressing,
  * video code, with BIOS emulation for VGA cards and a driver for TGA (21030),
  * Linux kernel code, both real and "ersatz",
  * Linux block device drivers,
  * file system support for ext2, MS-DOS, and ISO 9660,
  * the user interface,
  * kernel interface code, which sets up the HWRPB and memory map for Linux,
  * NVRAM code for environment variables.

=== PALcode ===

MILO's PALcode is based on the sample PALcode in Digital Semiconductor's Evaluation Board System Developers Kit (EBSDK). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-September.mbox#L11650|"Re: DEC PWS 433a(Miata) clock problem"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 14 Sep 1997)] The loader that starts MILO from ARC switches from the Windows NT PALcode to MILO's. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 5.1)] Each board has its own PALcode. Systems not built by Digital Semiconductor, such as the Avanti and Mikasa, use modified evaluation board PALcode. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L46991|"Re: Can't get my PC164 to boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Nov 1996)]

The EBSDK PALcode is less complete than the SRM PALcode. In 2004, Jay Estabrook wrote: "We prefer SRM whenever possible, for now and forever, because the PALcode that comes with it is "production quality". The PALcode that MILO provides [...] was based on EBSDK PALcode, which was supplied as-is for informational purpopses only". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-February.mbox#L913|"XL-266 install"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Feb 2004)] It lacks access to the performance counters and support for the prefetch code generated by Compaq's C compiler, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L38911|"RE: srm / ide"]], John Jemiolo, axp-list, 29 Feb 2000)] ECC error reporting, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-January.mbox#L13340|"Re: EB164 SRM Problems - Followup"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 29 Jan 2003)] and the PAL calls needed by NetBSD and OpenBSD. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-November.mbox#L52015|"Re: XP1000 & MILO & SCSI"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 27 Nov 2000)] MILO also does not provide the SRM console callbacks. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-December.mbox#L2306|"Re: Milo Development"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 1 Dec 1997)]

On many systems, the MILO and SRM PALcode deliver interrupts differently, so kernels built for one would not boot from the other. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L45013|"FAQ: MILO gets memory size wrong"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Aug 1998)] On the Noname, UDB, Avanti, and Platform 2000, the same kernel boots from both. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-May.mbox#L29482|"Re: 2.1.40 wont boot on Noname"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 May 1997)] Generic kernels, which run on either, came later. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L62453|"GENERIC kernels (was: Re: disparities in hardware?)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 30 Dec 1998)]

=== Device drivers and file systems ===

MILO is linked with the block device drivers of the Linux kernel it is built against. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 2)] Its file system code, however, is not the kernel's. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-September.mbox#L30442|"Re: MILO boot issues"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 19 Sep 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L2109|"MILO status (was Re: Test boot floppies (from CVS) and others)"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 9 Feb 2000)] MILO 2.2 can be built with ext2, MS-DOS, ISO 9660, and ReiserFS support. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/config.in|config.in]], MILO 2.2)] MILO does not configure network devices. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L9820|"Re: Red Hat Linux/Alpha 4.0 Available Now! (fwd)"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 8 Oct 1996)]

=== Video ===

MILO uses a VGA card if one is present, otherwise a TGA card. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 2)] VGA cards are initialized by running their x86 BIOS in an emulator. There were two: a free emulator, which "never did a good job with a number of cards", and Digital's emulator from its consoles, which was only available as a binary. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L35725|"Re: Milo problems."]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Oct 1996)] Digital's emulator was C++ code built on Red Hat 4.2, and did not link with anything newer, so building MILO with it required that system. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L16560|"Re: Qlogic ISP1040 & Digital Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Jun 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L56302|"Re: LX164 and rpcc"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Dec 1998)]

=== HWRPB and rebooting ===

MILO builds the HWRPB and memory cluster descriptions that tell Linux the system type and the available memory. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 2)] Because it must describe the system itself, there is a separate MILO for each platform. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L66835|"Re: /proc/cpuinfo"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 29 Jun 1998)] Early MILOs set the system serial number to ''Linux_is_Great!''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L32014|"Re: Linux_is_Great!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Dec 1998)] Later ones set it to ''MILO-'' followed by the version, e.g. ''MILO-2.0.35-c7'', which lets Linux tools detect that MILO was used. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L3844|"Re: Alpha issues (SRM installation)"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 11 Feb 2000)] The UP1000's APB loader also uses ''MILO-0000''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L466|"Re: MILO size problems"]], Rich Payne, debian-alpha, 3 Mar 2000)]

MILO leaves a compressed copy of itself in memory. When Linux reboots, it returns to that copy, which boots the same kernel with the same command after a 30 second timeout. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.3)] It is not possible to return to ARC. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-July.mbox#L28154|"Re: Rebooting UDB w/ RedHat 4.1"]], David A Rusling, axp-list, 28 Jul 1997)]

[(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-September.mbox#L5923|"Alpha Core ISO Images"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 29 Sep 2004)]

==== Loading MILO ====

MILO can be loaded from ARC, AlphaBIOS, the SRM console, an evaluation board Debug Monitor, a fail-safe boot block floppy, or flash. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 5)] The build produces ''milo'' for booting from ARC or AlphaBIOS, ''milo.dd'' for writing to a floppy and booting from SRM, and ''milo.rom'' for flashing. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-May.mbox#L19565|"Re: Building MILO (2.0.30)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 May 1997)]

=== From ARC or AlphaBIOS ===

ARC and AlphaBIOS run ''linload.exe'', which passes the memory size and boot selection from the firmware to MILO and switches to MILO's PALcode. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-April.mbox#L50716|"Re: MILO and PCI and BIOS ... gory details >LONG<"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 30 Apr 1998)] ''linload.exe'' was built with the Windows NT ARC firmware tree and Microsoft tools, and its source was never released. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-September.mbox#L466|"ARC/Linload/MILO/Linux - a maze of twisty little packages"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 2 Sep 1996)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L43156|"Re: DeskStation/Ruffian booting methods"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Jan 1999)] See [[software:firmware:arc#booting_milo|ARC]] and [[software:firmware:arc#booting_milo1|AlphaBIOS]] for the boot selection settings.

  * ''linload.exe'' and the MILO image must be on a FAT file system: a floppy, or a small FAT partition at the start of the disk. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-May.mbox#L60487|"Re: Floppyless boot on SX164"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 28 May 1999)] The files are copied to the floppy, not written with ''rawrite''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-October.mbox#L68745|"Re: AS 200 machines from onsale"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 7 Oct 1998)] The disk must therefore have a PC partition table, not a BSD disklabel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L59698|"Re: Questions."]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 1998)]
  * The contents of ''OSLOADOPTIONS'' (the OS options in AlphaBIOS) are run by MILO as a command, e.g. ''boot sda2:vmlinux.gz root=/dev/sda2'', which allows the system to boot unattended. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L18403|"Re: RedHat Install Help Please"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 16 Jan 1998)] Only one command can be given this way. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-November.mbox#L24237|"Re: Autoboot Linux/Alpha using milo-2029-pc164 ?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Nov 1997)]
  * ''OSLOADFILENAME'' names the MILO image, so several MILO versions can be kept on the same partition. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L19840|"Re: RedHat Install Help Please"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 16 Jan 1998)] Keeping a boot selection that only starts MILO is useful for testing. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L55416|"Re: Kernel compile issue with respect to PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 22 Jul 1998)]
  * The ''linload.exe'' version must match MILO, or MILO gets the memory size wrong. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-March.mbox#L23716|"Re: mem= option"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Mar 1997)] AlphaBIOS requires ''linload.exe'' 1.3 or later, and the Alpha XLT 366 the latest version. [(>linload 1.3 ''README'', Linux-Alpha archive, ftp.digital.com, 19 Dec 1996)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-February.mbox#L6604|"Re: Booting XLT-366 with 256 meg?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 6 Feb 1997)]
  * MILO can be started from the fail-safe booter by renaming it to ''fwupdate.exe'' on a FAT floppy and setting the fail-safe boot jumper. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L65937|"Re: AlphaPC 164SX, AlphaBIOS, and SRM"]], Oleg Gusev, axp-list, 22 Mar 1999)]

=== From SRM ===

The SRM console loads a contiguous image described by the boot block in the first sector of a disk. ''milo.dd'' is such an image, and is written to a floppy with ''dd'' and booted with ''boot dva0''. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 5.6)] According to Nikita Schmidt, "MILO itself *is* SRM bootable, the only thing that is missing is the bootblock". A boot block can be added with ''srmbootfat'' for a FAT floppy, or with ''objstrip -v -p milo mboot; cat mboot milo > milo.dd''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-September.mbox#L189|"Re: MILO"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 4 Sep 1998)] MILO can also be booted from a small raw partition on a hard disk. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L60139|"Re: AS 200 4/233 - MILO not an option?"]], Nikita Schmidt, axp-list, 25 Jun 1998)]

Booting MILO from SRM saves memory, since MILO's footprint is about 1 MiB smaller than SRM's. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L4976|"Re: Bad Debian/Alpha potato rescue floppy image?"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 28 Mar 2000)] However, SRM does not pass the memory size to MILO the way ''linload.exe'' does, so older MILOs used a default of 24 MiB on the Noname and UDB and 32 MiB on other systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L45013|"FAQ: MILO gets memory size wrong"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Aug 1998)] Newer MILOs detect the memory size when booted from SRM. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L18812|"Re: Mutia SRM reports 32MB RAM; Linux sees ~21MB"]], Nikita Schmidt, axp-list, 13 Oct 1999)] Where SRM is available, booting Linux directly with aboot is simpler. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L54844|"Re: Jakma,Paul and warp"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 30 Nov 1999)]

=== From flash ===

On the AlphaPC64, Noname, EB66+, EB164, and PC164, MILO can be written to flash and started directly. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 5.5)] This is done with the Flash Management Utility, which is started from MILO with ''run fd0:fmu.gz'' and contains its own copy of MILO, [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 7)] or from the Debug Monitor. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L31581|"Re: FMU.GZ crashing"]], David Mosberger-Tang, axp-list, 17 Nov 1996)] The ''bootopt'' command selects which flash image is started at power-on. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.4)] In the flash image header, MILO has firmware ID 7. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 7.2)]

Digital stopped supporting this in 1998. Jay Estabrook explained that "MILO was never intended to be the primary console software", and relies on the previous console for some hardware initialization. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L949|"Re: fmu on PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Jan 1998)] The Flash Management Utility was not kept up to date with MILO. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-October.mbox#L22001|"Re: Milo in Flash rom"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Oct 1998)] Some users overwrote the fail-safe booter in flash block 0. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-February.mbox#L24505|"fmu on pc164"]], Robert G. Smith, axp-list, 20 Feb 1997)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L39894|"Oops! Lost the Failsafe loader on LX164"]], Greg Johnson, axp-list, 16 Aug 1998)] MILO 2.2-18 added the ''makeflash'' utility, which writes a MILO image for the UDB that can be flashed with the SRM ''update'' command. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/ChangeLog|ChangeLog]], MILO 2.2-18, 19 Jul 2001)]

==== Commands ====

The MILO 2.2 commands are: [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/milo.c|milo.c]], MILO 2.2)]

^ Command ^ Description ^
| ''ls'' [//path//] | List files in a directory |
| ''cd'' //path// | Change the current directory |
| ''boot'' [//path// [//arguments//]] | Boot a kernel |
| ''read'' [//dev//'':''] //block// | Read a block from a device |
| ''run'' //path// | Run a standalone program |
| ''show'' | Display all known devices and file systems |
| ''sleep'' //seconds// | Wait |
| ''pci'' | Display the PCI configuration |
| ''set'' [//variable// //value//], ''unset'' //variable// | Set, display, or delete variables |
| ''setenv'', ''unsetenv'', ''resetenv'' | Set or delete variables stored in NVRAM, instead of ''set'' and ''unset'' on systems with NVRAM support |
| ''bootopt'' //num// | Select the firmware to start at power-on (Cabriolet, EB66+, EB164, and PC164 only) |
| ''help'' | Print the command summary |

  * Paths are given as //device//'':''//file//, with devices named as in Linux, e.g. ''fd0'', ''hda1'', ''sda2'', or ''scd0''. Devices are only initialized by the first ''show'', ''ls'', ''boot'', or ''run''. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.1)] Forgetting the colon can cause MILO to fail. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L19964|"Re: Trouble with installation"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 13 Oct 1999)]
  * Older MILOs take a ''-t'' option to select a file system other than ext2, e.g. ''ls -t iso9660 scd0:''. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.1)]
  * Commands can be abbreviated, e.g. ''b sda2:vmlinux.gz root=/dev/sda2''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-February.mbox#L3610|"New Milo images and source on gatekeeper.dec.com"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 15 Feb 1996)]
  * Adding ''| more'' to a command pages its output. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-October.mbox#L1802|"enhancements to MILO"]], Arnaud Installe, debian-alpha, 8 Oct 1998)]
  * MILO takes commands from both the keyboard and COM1. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-September.mbox#L30442|"Re: MILO boot issues"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 19 Sep 1998)]

==== Configuration ====

=== milo.conf ===

Nikita Schmidt's MILOs and MILO 2.2 read a configuration file similar to ''lilo.conf'' when given ''boot'' with only a device, e.g. ''boot sda2:''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L3919|"MILO status"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 11 Jul 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/tools/boot/milo.conf.5|milo.conf(5)]], MILO 2.2)] MILO 2.2 looks for ''/etc/milo.conf'', then ''/milo.cfg''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/milo.c|milo.c]], MILO 2.2)]

<code>
timeout = 50
command = "set MEMORY_SIZE 224"

image = /boot/vmlinux.gz
  label = linux
  root = /dev/sda2
  append = "console=ttyS0"
</code>

  * ''timeout'' is in tenths of a second. It boots the first image; 0 means no time limit. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/tools/boot/milo.conf.5|milo.conf(5)]], MILO 2.2)] Michal Jaegermann reported in 1999 that ''timeout'' did not work in any MILO he had seen. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-August.mbox#L10363|"Re: running a multia w/o a monitor and keyboard"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 9 Aug 1999)]
  * ''command'' runs a MILO command when the file is read, or, inside an image section, just before booting that image. It can be repeated. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/tools/boot/milo.conf.5|milo.conf(5)]], MILO 2.2)]
  * Each ''image'' starts a section, with ''label'' (case sensitive), ''root'', ''append'', and ''device''. The image can include a device, e.g. ''sda1:/vmlinux.gz''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/tools/boot/milo.conf.5|milo.conf(5)]], MILO 2.2)]
  * At the MILO prompt, ''boot sda2:'' followed by a label boots that image. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-July.mbox#L6347|"Re: Finally booted RH6, but [p|th]ings still go wrong"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 5 Jul 1999)]

=== miloctl ===

''miloctl'' edits the parameter block inside a MILO image file (not the copy in memory): [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/tools/boot/miloctl.8|miloctl(8)]], Nikita Schmidt, MILO 2.2)]

  * ''cmd=''//command// sets a startup command, run when MILO is about to show its prompt, with one second to abort it. ''OSLOADOPTIONS'' overrides it. A typical startup command is ''boot sda2:'', which reads ''milo.conf''.
  * ''mem=''//size// overrides the memory size in MiB.
  * ''+S'' and ''+V'' set the ''SERIAL_OUTPUT'' and ''VERBOSE'' flags, which are off by default.

For example, ''miloctl -v /dev/fd0 +S +V'' enables output to the serial port during startup on a MILO floppy. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/2000-December.mbox#L489|"Re: [Milo-list] RE: Milo problem on Alpha XL 300"]], Nikita Schmidt, milo-list, 5 Dec 2000)] A startup command and ''milo.conf'' can be combined by using the startup command only to point to ''milo.conf''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L890|"Re: Milo - question"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 4 Jun 1999)]

==== Booting Linux ====

A kernel is booted with ''boot'' //device//'':''//file// followed by the kernel arguments, e.g.: [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.2)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L38661|"Re: RH 6.1 installer"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Dec 1999)]

<code>
MILO> boot sda2:vmlinux.gz root=/dev/sda2
MILO> boot fd0:vmlinux.gz root=/dev/fd0 load_ramdisk=1
MILO> boot sr0:kernels/generic.gz root=/dev/scd0 text
</code>

  * The kernel can be compressed with gzip. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 6.2)] In December 1996, David Rusling announced that MILO recognizes compressed and uncompressed ELF and COFF kernels without a ''.gz'' suffix. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-December.mbox#L17442|"New MILO sources..."]], David Rusling, axp-list, 11 Dec 1996)] MILO 2.2 only loads ELF kernels. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/ChangeLog|ChangeLog]], MILO 2.2, 25 Nov 1999)]
  * MILO's version number is the kernel it was built against, not the kernels it can boot. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-April.mbox#L16381|"Re: Milo/Alpha/Kernel"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Apr 1998)] In practice old MILOs cannot boot some newer kernels:
    * MILOs without ELF support cannot load ELF kernels, which are required since Linux 2.1.0. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L10972|"Re: Kernel 2.1.7"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 5 Nov 1996)]
    * Old MILOs could not load large 2.1 kernels. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-May.mbox#L22451|"Re: 2.1.xx and PC164's"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 May 1998)] The old Ruffian ''milo.ful'' only loads kernels smaller than about 700 KiB. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L54291|"Re: ldmilo problem on ruffian"]], Ron Farrer, axp-list, 26 Apr 2001)]
    * Linux 2.1.131 and later need a MILO that reports the system variation in the HWRPB. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L14839|"Re: Kernel 2.1.131 compiles but hangs..."]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 6 Dec 1998)] Richard Henderson reportedly said: "I never knew there were so many broken MILOs out there." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L15370|"RE: booting 2.2.1 on a serial console"]], dr john halewood, axp-list, 9 Feb 1999)]
    * Linux 2.4 moved the kernel start address from 0x310000 to 0x810000, to make room for the larger SRM console on Wildfire systems. Old MILOs hardcode the old address, so kernels for them need ''CONFIG_ALPHA_LEGACY_START_ADDRESS''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-June.mbox#L18501|"Re: Linux-2.4.0-test2"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 24 Jun 2000)] MILO 2.2-17 and later take the address from the ELF headers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/ChangeLog|ChangeLog]], MILO 2.2, 26 Jun 2000)] Jay Estabrook still recommended the legacy start address "for *all* MILO machine kernels". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-September.mbox#L6740|"Re: Woody, Milo, XLT and 2.4.22"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 16 Sep 2003)] Debian later provided a separate legacy kernel flavor for MILO systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2006-February.mbox#L7632|"Bug#352186: LEGACY_START should not be set on alpha"]], Steve Langasek, debian-alpha, 10 Feb 2006)]
    * Kernels from Linux 2.6.22 have a ''PT_NOTE'' program header, which MILO did not handle until 2026. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/commit/dd79b34|"fix(boot): skip non-PT_LOAD segments when loading kernel image"]], alphalinux-org/milo)]
  * **initrd:** MILO 2.2-17 and later pass an initial ramdisk with ''initrd=''//path// on the command line. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-January.mbox#L14660|"Re: Upgrading my XL266 from 6.2 to 7.0 MILO"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 11 Jan 2001)] The path has no device, since MILO only has one device open at a time. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L11600|"Re: initrd problems"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 11 Apr 2002)] Initrd images larger than 2 MiB could not be loaded. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-October.mbox#L12790|"Re: Double trouble - NFS don't work, and then 2.2.19 -> 2.4.18 woes"]], Herbert Xu, debian-alpha, 19 Oct 2002)]
  * Linux kernels built to boot from MILO must not be configured to use SRM as the boot loader. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-November.mbox#L288|"Re: SCSI broken on 2.4.18?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 2 Nov 2002)]
  * When booted from ARC or AlphaBIOS, the system clock uses the ARC epoch. See [[software:firmware:arc#known_issues|ARC known issues]].

==== Building ====

MILO is built against a configured and compiled Linux kernel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L45466|"Re: milo (it's me again)"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 25 Nov 1996)] MILO 2.2 needs a generic Linux 2.2 kernel, at least 2.2.18, without modules or sysctl support. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/README.milo|README.milo]], MILO 2.2)] The repository now includes the Linux 2.2.26 sources, and builds for the Miata. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/commit/e5a4a2a|"vendor Linux 2.2.26 sources and fix MIATA cross-compilation"]], alphalinux-org/milo)]

Building MILO was difficult for most of its history. The PALcode could only be assembled with the old a.out assembler from the evaluation board tools, so prebuilt PALcode was included. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 8)] The 2.0 MILOs required building a kernel for every system. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-June.mbox#L2630|"Re: rebuilding MILO images from source"]], David Huggins-Daines, debian-alpha, 18 Jun 2000)] In 2007, Steve Langasek wrote that the source was "just not buildable with a modern toolchain." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2007-November.mbox#L4736|"Re: miata linux kernel"]], Steve Langasek, debian-alpha, 19 Nov 2007)]

==== Licensing ====

MILO's ''LICENSE'' file contains three licenses: Digital's PALcode license, which allows use only in products with an Alpha processor; a license for the BIOS emulation libraries, which forbids modifying them; and the GNU GPL. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/Documentation/LICENSE|LICENSE]], MILO 2.2)] Most MILO source files carry a separate Digital notice that permits use, copying, modification, and distribution. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/milo/blob/main/milo.c|milo.c]], MILO 2.2)] Stefan Reinauer noted that the ''LICENSE'' file "seems to state that everything except the PALcode and the BIOS emulation is GPL", while the source headers "give not a single statement about GPL." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L446|"Re: Milo 2.2 - things are becoming generic."]], Stefan Reinauer, milo-list, 8 Sep 1999)] ''linload.exe'' is not free, since it uses parts of the Windows NT SDK. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L43156|"Re: DeskStation/Ruffian booting methods"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Jan 1999)]

==== Known issues ====

  * **Serial port:** most MILO builds echo output to COM1 and accept input from it. A modem or other device on COM1 fills the screen with ''O>'' and blocks input. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 9)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-September.mbox#L30442|"Re: MILO boot issues"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 19 Sep 1998)]
  * **Large partitions:** older MILOs fail to read a kernel that is more than 2 GiB into a partition, because ''ext2_iget()'' stores the offset in an ''int''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-September.mbox#L35301|"Re: Milo/kernel problem"]], Nikita Schmidt, axp-list, 21 Sep 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L19586|"Re: Kernel panic (was: RE: need help with tulip.c)"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 9 Jan 1999)] Older MILOs could also only handle 256 block groups. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L62725|"Re: What does this message mean??"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 24 Jul 1998)]
  * **ext2 features:** MILOs of 1999 cannot read revision 1 ext2 file systems, which have the file type in directory entries, or file systems with 4 KiB blocks. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L72|"Re: Milo 2.2 (release)"]], Matt Wilson, milo-list, 15 Nov 1999)] These became the defaults of ''mke2fs''. ''mke2fs -O none'' creates a file system that MILO can read. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L52432|"Re: Red Hat Rawhide/Alpha"]], Bill Nottingham, axp-list, 29 Nov 1999)] Debian's boot floppies forced 1 KiB blocks on MILO systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-March.mbox#L751|"Re: New boot floppies up for testing"]], Falk Hueffner, debian-alpha, 18 Mar 2002)] Another workaround is to keep the kernel on the FAT partition next to MILO. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2001-August.mbox#L7288|"Re: (alpha) YES, we have working boot-floppies"]], Falk Hueffner, debian-alpha, 21 Aug 2001)] MILO reads ext3 as ext2, ignoring the journal. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L9840|"Re: 7.2 on XL366 - help!"]], Stefan Reinauer, axp-list, 31 Oct 2003)] It cannot read XFS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L38559|"SGI XFS Red Hat 7.1 Installer for Alpha"]], Martin K. Petersen, axp-list, 30 Jul 2001)]
  * **VGA behind a PCI bridge:** MILO cannot initialize a VGA card behind a PCI-to-PCI bridge, e.g. in the 32-bit slots of the AlphaPC164UX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L26968|"Re: PC164UX doesnt work - anyone got details"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Aug 1998)]
  * **24-plane TGA:** released MILO sources did not support 24-plane TGA cards, and gave a green screen on the AlphaStation 200. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L5547|"Re: MILO status"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 21 Jul 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L893|"Re: I just bought an Alpha. Now what?"]], Matthew R. Briggs, debian-alpha, 13 Aug 1998)]
  * **SCSI controllers:** a controller can only be used if its driver is built into MILO. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, section 9)] Older MILOs lacked support for Adaptec Ultra2 controllers, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L49885|"Re: Linux + Adaptec 2940U2W = ??"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 1999)] and MILO 2.2-18 hangs loading the driver for an AHA-2940U2 on the SX164. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-August.mbox#L12509|"Milo 2.2-18 hangs loading Adptec 2940 U2 SCSI adapter on SX164"]], Ram Viswanadha, axp-list, 22 Aug 2002)] MILO cannot be booted from a BusLogic controller, because ARC and AlphaBIOS do not support them. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-June.mbox#L15694|"Re: Any1 with a working PC164!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 13 Jun 1997)] Driver options cannot be passed to MILO's drivers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-May.mbox#L17155|"Re: milo and 4.3 GB scsi drive"]], Michal Jaegermann, axp-list, 24 May 2000)]
  * **MILO 2.2 on older systems:** 2.2 MILOs were mainly tested on the PC164LX and Ruffian, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L8841|"Re: Test boot floppies (from CVS)"]], David Huggins-Daines, debian-alpha, 31 Jan 2000)] and users reported that they did not work on the XLT, PC164, or SX164. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L8993|"Re: Test boot floppies (from CVS)"]], Paul Slootman, debian-alpha, 1 Feb 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L27381|"RE: milo for PC164"]], Heid Oliver, axp-list, 31 Jan 2002)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-March.mbox#L19626|"Re: binutils, modutils, and kernel (2.4.19-pre3)"]], Rajiv Prasad, axp-list, 17 Mar 2002)] Some 2.2 binaries were built with ''-mcpu=ev56'' and do not run on EV4 or EV5 systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L2109|"MILO status (was Re: Test boot floppies (from CVS) and others)"]], Nikita Schmidt, debian-alpha, 9 Feb 2000)]
  * **Ruffian ldmilo:** ''ldmilo.exe'' failed a hardware check on AlphaPC164UX boards made by Deskstation, though not on those made by Samsung. Jay Estabrook produced a patched ''ldmilo.exe'' that skips the check. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L55538|"Re: ldmilo problem on ruffian"]], Ron Farrer, axp-list, 28 Apr 2001)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L4764|"Re: Alpha newbie install woes"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 4 Apr 2002)]
  * **Security:** the PALcode in the MILOs shipped with Red Hat 5.x included the debugging PAL call ''NPHALT'', which let any user halt the machine to the console. MILOs from ''genie.ucd.ie'' were fixed. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L14495|"Milo bugtraq alert"]], Daniel J. Frasnelli, axp-list, 7 Feb 1999)]
  * **Low power idle:** MILO's PALcode generally lacks the ''wtint'' call, which Nikita Schmidt added only for the Avanti. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L23394|"Low power idle mode patch"]], Nikita Schmidt, axp-list, 15 Oct 1999)]
