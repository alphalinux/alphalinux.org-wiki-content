===== Tru64 UNIX =====

Tru64 UNIX was Digital's UNIX operating system for Alpha. Early versions were called DEC OSF/1. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_1000:alphaserver_1000_owners_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 1000 Owner's Guide}}, p. 3-1)] It was based on the Open Software Foundation's OSF/1 Release 1.0.4, with the virtual memory system heavily modified. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-osf1-mbox/googlegroups-1993-April.mbox#L2838|"Re: wither OSF? (was Re: alpha OSF/1 1.2)"]], Tom Jordahl, comp.unix.osf.osf1, 14 Apr 1993)] By the time OSF announced in 1994 that its June 1994 release of OSF/1 would be its last, Digital's development no longer depended on OSF code. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-osf1-mbox/googlegroups-1995-August.mbox#L8638|"DEC OSF/1 AXP Frequently Asked Questions"]], Steve Lionel, comp.unix.osf.osf1, 21 Aug 1995)] V1.0 ran on MIPS DECstations, and V1.2 was the version that shipped on Alpha; [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-misc-mbox/googlegroups-1992-November.mbox#L514|"Re: Is DEC OSF stable?"]], Stephen Carpenter, comp.unix.osf.misc, 24 Nov 1992)] the DECstation version, meant to replace ULTRIX, was cancelled before any official release. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-misc-mbox/googlegroups-2000-March.mbox#L2139|"RESULT: comp.unix.tru64 rename and removal of comp.unix.osf.misc pass"]], Neil Crellin, comp.unix.osf.misc, 21 Mar 2000)]

The system was renamed Digital UNIX on 14 March 1995, when Digital received an X/Open license to use the UNIX trademark for the release then current, which became Digital UNIX Version 3.2. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/biz-digital-announce-mbox/googlegroups-1995-March.mbox#L2459|"Press/Digital To Rename DEC OSF/1 Operating Systems 'Digital UNIX'"]], Digital Press and Analysts News, biz.digital.announce, 15 Mar 1995)] Compaq announced the rename of Digital UNIX to Tru64 UNIX on 1 February 1999, together with V4.0F, the AlphaServer DS20, and the XP1000. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L27502|"News/announcement: Compaq Tru64 UNIX and new Web site.."]], Tom Blinn, alpha-osf-managers, 1 Feb 1999)] [(>''Tru64 UNIX Operating System, Version 4.0F'', SPD 41.61.22, Compaq, April 1999)]

==== Supported systems ====

The Tru64 UNIX V4.0F Software Product Description lists these supported systems: [(>''Tru64 UNIX Operating System, Version 4.0F'', SPD 41.61.22, Compaq, April 1999, Hardware Support Tables)]

  * DEC 2000, DEC 3000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000
  * AlphaServer 300, 400, 800, 1000, 1000A, 1200, 2000, 2100, 2100A, 4000, 4100, 8200, 8400, DS20, ES40, GS60, and GS140
  * AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, 400, 500, 600, and 600A, the Personal Workstation au series, and the Professional Workstation XP1000
  * the [[hardware:motherboards:axppci33|AXPpci 33]], [[hardware:motherboards:alpha_vme|AXPvme and Alpha VME]] boards, and the single board computers EB66+, EB164, AlphaPC 64, AlphaPC 164, AlphaPC 164LX, and AlphaPC 164SX

The list omits some systems that V4.0F did support, such as the AlphaServer DS10, [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds10_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS10 V7.3 Console Firmware Release Notes}})] so the absence of a system from it does not show that the system was unsupported. Which operating systems each system supports is summarized in [[software:operating_systems#support_by_system|Operating Systems: Support by system]].

==== End of support for older systems ====

In 2000 Compaq announced that V5.1 would be the last release to support the older DEC-branded systems without PCI buses (the DEC 2000, DEC 3000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000/10000), and recommended V4.0G to their owners because of its planned long service life. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L10241|"Tru64 UNIX V5.1 and TruCluster software"]], Tom Blinn, tru64-unix-managers, 20 Sep 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L37862|"end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], William H. Magill, axp-list, 20 Sep 2000)] Support for the TURBOchannel systems ended one release earlier. By 2002 support for them had been dropped in V5.1 and later, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-May.mbox#L46415|"Summary: Firmware revision and tru64 version lifelimit on Alpha 1 000A 5/400 and Alpha 800"]], Mike Kirkland, tru64-unix-managers, 30 May 2002)] and V5.0A was the last release supported on the DEC 3000; V5.1 ran on it without support, and V5.1A, from which all TURBOchannel code was removed, did not run at all. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2003-January.mbox#L30858|"SUMMARY: DEC3000 300s and DEC 3000 400s with Tru64 v5"]], Robert Bannocks, tru64-unix-managers, 30 Jan 2003)] The AlphaServer 8200 and 8400 remained supported after V5.1, but without their XMI bus. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L39979|"Re: end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 22 Sep 2000)] The EV7 systems require V5.1B. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es47:alphaserver_es47_tower_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer ES47 Tower QuickSpecs}}, November 2003, p. 2)]

==== Licensing ====

The base license included a C compiler, but only for rebuilding and configuring the kernel; general development needed the separate Developers' Extensions (later Developers' Toolkit) license. The only enforcement was a license check in the ''dbx'' and DECladebug debuggers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-misc-mbox/googlegroups-1995-June.mbox#L1053|"Re: Really quick question on OSF1 ?"]], Jon Reeves, comp.unix.osf.misc, 19 Jun 1995)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-osf1-mbox/googlegroups-1994-September.mbox#L25101|"Re: Any compiler in the OSF/1"]], Steve Lionel, comp.unix.osf.osf1, 1 Sep 1994)] The compilers are described in [[documentation:toolchains|Toolchains]]. Each release shipped with the Alpha AXP Freeware CD of ported free software. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-osf1-mbox/googlegroups-1994-December.mbox#L6409|"DEC OSF/1 AXP Frequently Asked Questions"]], Steve Lionel, comp.unix.osf.osf1, 16 Dec 1994)] Universities could license the source code, which also required licenses from The Open Group because the system included sources derived from both System V and OSF. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-misc-mbox/googlegroups-1998-June.mbox#L1341|"Re: DIGITAL UNIX SOURCE CODE"]], Jeffrey Mogul, comp.unix.osf.misc, 30 Jun 1998)]

Tru64 UNIX could be licensed for non-commercial use, as OpenVMS could through its hobbyist program. A complete non-commercial kit cost US$99 from Compaq in 2001, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2001-December.mbox#L15119|"Re: emulator for tru64"]], Oisin McGuinness, comp.unix.tru64, 6 Dec 2001)] and under HP in 2003 roughly US$100 bought the current Tru64 release together with the HP C compiler for Tru64. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2003-July.mbox#L17105|"Free UNIX for non-commerical use."]], David Kirkby, comp.unix.tru64, 27 Jul 2003)]

==== Under HP ====

After the HP and Compaq merger, HP's roadmap of 7 May 2002 made HP-UX its UNIX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-May.mbox#L9640|"HPs roadmap for HPQ..."]], Arrigo Triulzi, tru64-unix-managers, 8 May 2002)] The plan was to carry the most valued Tru64 features, AdvFS and TruCluster, over to HP-UX, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-June.mbox#L27456|"SUMMARY: HP Survey. If you use both Tru64UNIX and HP-UX, what do you like about them?"]], Paul Henderson, tru64-unix-managers, 24 Jun 2002)] and by November 2002 the TruCluster engineers were porting TruCluster functionality to HP-UX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-November.mbox#L16953|"SUMMARY: handle advfs filesets with autofs ?"]], Udo Grabowski, tru64-unix-managers, 15 Nov 2002)] In December 2002 HP's public commitment was to manufacture and market Alpha systems running Tru64 UNIX through about 2006 and to support Tru64 UNIX on Alpha into about 2011, but not to port Tru64 UNIX to Itanium. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-December.mbox#L17609|"Summary: Some questions regarding Alpha and Itanium"]], Robert Honore, tru64-unix-managers, 19 Dec 2002)] V5.1B was to be supported until at least 2011. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2005-January.mbox#L2619|"SUMMARY: probleme booting a cluster member"]], Malek Shabou, tru64-unix-managers, 13 Jan 2005)] HP abandoned the port of Tru64 features to HP-UX in December 2004. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2004-December.mbox#L686|"OT but related IT appears HP has lied to us all"]], Greg Rudd, tru64-unix-managers, 3 Dec 2004)]

HP did not port ''vrestore'' to Linux, so ''vdump'' archives can only be restored on a Tru64 system; ''tar'', ''pax'', and ''cpio'' archives are portable to Linux. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2007-January.mbox#L2157|"SUMMARY: vrestore on linux"]], Emanuele Lombardi, tru64-unix-managers, 23 Jan 2007)]
