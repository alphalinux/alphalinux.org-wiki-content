===== Tru64 UNIX =====

Tru64 UNIX was the UNIX operating system for Alpha from Digital, and later from Compaq and HP. It was called DEC OSF/1 until 1995 and Digital UNIX until 1999. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_1000:alphaserver_1000_owners_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 1000 Owner's Guide}}, p. 3-1)] The first Alpha release, DEC OSF/1 V1.2, reached customers in March 1993, and the last, V5.1B, in late 2002, followed by patch releases up to V5.1B-6 (see [[#releases|Releases]]). After acquiring Compaq, HP made HP-UX its UNIX [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-May.mbox#L9640|"HPs roadmap for HPQ..."]], Arrigo Triulzi, tru64-unix-managers, 8 May 2002)] and did not port Tru64 UNIX to Itanium. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-December.mbox#L17609|"Summary: Some questions regarding Alpha and Itanium"]], Robert Honore, tru64-unix-managers, 19 Dec 2002)] HP's engineering support ended at the end of 2012, after which it offered Mature Product Support without further releases. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2011-April.mbox#L1264|"Re: Tru64 licenses"]], Martin, comp.unix.tru64, 23 Apr 2011)]

==== Tru64 UNIX and Linux ====

Tru64 UNIX and Linux can be installed on the same machine, since both boot from the SRM console; see [[software:operating_systems#dual_booting|Operating Systems: Dual booting]], which also covers installing [[software:boot_loaders:aboot|aboot]] on a disk labeled by Tru64 UNIX.

HP did not port ''vrestore'' to Linux, so ''vdump'' archives can only be restored on a Tru64 system; ''tar'', ''pax'', and ''cpio'' archives are portable to Linux. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2007-January.mbox#L2157|"SUMMARY: vrestore on linux"]], Emanuele Lombardi, tru64-unix-managers, 23 Jan 2007)] Linux reads Tru64 UNIX disk labels and UFS file systems, but not AdvFS; see [[software:operating_systems#exchanging_files|Operating Systems: Exchanging files]].

Linux 5.17 and earlier could run some Tru64 UNIX programs, such as Netscape and Acrobat, through the kernel's a.out and ECOFF binary support together with Tru64 UNIX shared libraries. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/fs/Kconfig.binfmt?h=v5.17|fs/Kconfig.binfmt]], Linux 5.17)] Linux 5.18 stopped building that support on Alpha, [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/19e8b701e258|"a.out: Stop building a.out/osf1 support on alpha and m68k"]], Linux 5.18)] and Linux 6.1 removed it. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/987f20a9dcce|"a.out: Remove the a.out implementation"]], Linux 6.1)] Current kernels keep the OSF/1 system calls in the Alpha system call table, but cannot load Tru64 UNIX programs. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/syscalls/syscall.tbl?h=v7.3-rc1|arch/alpha/kernel/syscalls/syscall.tbl]], Linux 7.3)]

==== Origins and names ====

DEC OSF/1 was based on the Open Software Foundation's OSF/1 Release 1.0.4. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-osf-osf1-mbox/googlegroups-1993-April.mbox#L2838|"Re: wither OSF? (was Re: alpha OSF/1 1.2)"]], Tom Jordahl, comp.unix.osf.osf1, 14 Apr 1993)] It was renamed Digital UNIX in March 1995, with Version 3.2, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/biz-digital-announce-mbox/googlegroups-1995-March.mbox#L2459|"Press/Digital To Rename DEC OSF/1 Operating Systems 'Digital UNIX'"]], Digital Press and Analysts News, biz.digital.announce, 15 Mar 1995)] and Tru64 UNIX in February 1999, with V4.0F. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L27502|"News/announcement: Compaq Tru64 UNIX and new Web site.."]], Tom Blinn, alpha-osf-managers, 1 Feb 1999)]

==== Supported systems ====

The Tru64 UNIX V4.0F Software Product Description lists these supported systems: [(>''Tru64 UNIX Operating System, Version 4.0F'', SPD 41.61.22, Compaq, April 1999, Hardware Support Tables)]

  * DEC 2000, DEC 3000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000
  * AlphaServer 300, 400, 800, 1000, 1000A, 1200, 2000, 2100, 2100A, 4000, 4100, 8200, 8400, DS20, ES40, GS60, and GS140
  * AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, 400, 500, 600, and 600A, the Personal Workstation au series, and the Professional Workstation XP1000
  * the [[hardware:motherboards:axppci33|AXPpci 33]], [[hardware:motherboards:alpha_vme|AXPvme and Alpha VME]] boards, and the single board computers EB66+, EB164, AlphaPC 64, AlphaPC 164, AlphaPC 164LX, and AlphaPC 164SX

The list omits some systems that V4.0F did support, such as the AlphaServer DS10, [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds10_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS10 V7.3 Console Firmware Release Notes}})]. Which operating systems each system supports is summarized in [[software:operating_systems#support_by_system|Operating Systems: Support by system]].

==== End of support for older systems ====

V5.1 is the last release supported on the older DEC-branded systems without PCI buses (the DEC 2000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000/10000); Compaq recommended V4.0G to their owners because of that release's planned long service life. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L10241|"Tru64 UNIX V5.1 and TruCluster software"]], Tom Blinn, tru64-unix-managers, 20 Sep 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L37862|"end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], William H. Magill, axp-list, 20 Sep 2000)] On the TURBOchannel DEC 3000, V5.0A is the last supported release; [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-May.mbox#L46415|"Summary: Firmware revision and tru64 version lifelimit on Alpha 1 000A 5/400 and Alpha 800"]], Mike Kirkland, tru64-unix-managers, 30 May 2002)] V5.1 runs on it without support, and V5.1A, from which all TURBOchannel code was removed, does not run. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2003-January.mbox#L30858|"SUMMARY: DEC3000 300s and DEC 3000 400s with Tru64 v5"]], Robert Bannocks, tru64-unix-managers, 30 Jan 2003)]

==== Licensing ====

Compaq, and later HP, sold inexpensive licenses and media for non-commercial use, comparable to the OpenVMS hobbyist program. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2001-December.mbox#L15119|"Re: emulator for tru64"]], Oisin McGuinness, comp.unix.tru64, 6 Dec 2001)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2003-July.mbox#L17105|"Free UNIX for non-commerical use."]], David Kirkby, comp.unix.tru64, 27 Jul 2003)] By 2011 the program had ended, and HP no longer sold hobbyist licenses. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-unix-tru64-mbox/googlegroups-2011-April.mbox#L695|"Re: Tru64 licenses"]], Kari Uusimäki, comp.unix.tru64, 23 Apr 2011)]
