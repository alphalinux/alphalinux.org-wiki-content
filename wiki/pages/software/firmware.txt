===== Firmware =====

Alpha systems use one of two kinds of firmware, depending on the operating system they were designed for. Some systems carry both and can switch between them.

^ Firmware ^ Interface ^ Operating systems ^ Linux boot loader ^
| [[software:firmware:srm|SRM console]] | Command line | OpenVMS, Digital UNIX (Tru64 UNIX), Linux, NetBSD, OpenBSD | [[software:boot_loaders:aboot|aboot]] |
| [[software:firmware:arc|ARC, AlphaBIOS, and ARCSBIOS]] | Menus | Windows NT, Linux | [[software:boot_loaders:milo|MILO]] (APB on the UP1000) |

See also [[hardware:motherboards:motherboards|Motherboards]], whose pages describe the firmware available for each board.

==== Documentation ====

See also the documentation sections of the [[software:firmware:srm|SRM]] and [[software:firmware:arc|ARC]] pages.
