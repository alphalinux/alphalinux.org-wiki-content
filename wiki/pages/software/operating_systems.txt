===== Operating Systems =====

Digital supported three operating systems on Alpha: OpenVMS, DEC OSF/1 (renamed Digital UNIX and then Tru64 UNIX), and Microsoft Windows NT. Linux, NetBSD, and OpenBSD were ported by others. Which of these a system can run depends mostly on its [[software:firmware|firmware]]:

^ Operating system ^ Firmware ^
| OpenVMS, Digital UNIX / Tru64 UNIX | [[software:firmware:srm|SRM console]] |
| Windows NT | [[software:firmware:arc|ARC or AlphaBIOS]] |
| Linux | SRM (with [[software:boot_loaders:aboot|aboot]]), or ARC, AlphaBIOS, or ARCSBIOS (with [[software:boot_loaders:milo|MILO]]) |
| NetBSD, OpenBSD | SRM |

"All motherboard systems that run the DIGITAL UNIX or OpenVMS operating system require the Alpha SRM Console." [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:common:srm_console_user_guide.pdf?linkonly|Alpha SRM Console for Alpha Microprocessor Motherboards User's Guide}}, p. 1-2)] Systems designed only for Windows NT, such as the Alpha XL, have no SRM console, and so cannot run OpenVMS or Tru64 UNIX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L25578|"Re: Does DEC have a clue?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Jan 1998)] The SRM console for evaluation boards and OEM motherboards was licensed separately (see [[software:firmware:srm#licensing|SRM licensing]]).

==== Windows NT ====

Windows NT ran on Alpha from version 3.1. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2006-December.mbox#L1768|"Alpha Wiki"]], Marco Benton, axp-list, 18 Dec 2006)] The DECpc 150 AXP was bundled with Windows NT, and the otherwise identical DEC 2000 AXP with OpenVMS or DEC OSF/1. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L4787|"Jensen-HOWTO: First pass"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 15 Mar 1996)] Digital's workstations were sold in Windows NT and UNIX/OpenVMS versions, e.g. the Personal Workstation "a" series with Windows NT 4.0 and "au" series with Tru64 UNIX or OpenVMS. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:personal_workstation:personal_workstation_a_au_series_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq DPW Enhanced a-Series & au-Series}}, Systems and Options Catalog, September 2000)] Digital Semiconductor's evaluation boards and OEM motherboards shipped with Windows NT firmware, and supported Windows NT 3.51 and 4.0. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:common:windows_nt_installation_guide.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Microprocessors Motherboard Windows NT 3.51 and 4.0 Installation Guide}}, p. 28)]

In August 1999, Compaq laid off the more than 100 people who ported Windows NT to Alpha. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-August.mbox#L31035|"Re: Done: Experience with large IDE drive and Promise Ultra/66 card"]], Jeff Bolle, axp-list, 22 Aug 1999)] According to Phillip Ezolt of Compaq, Compaq stopped supporting 32-bit Windows NT, and within a week Microsoft dropped the 64-bit version. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L54447|"Re: Off topic: NT on Alpha (was: Alpha Users in the Toronto area.)"]], Phillip Ezolt, axp-list, 30 Sep 1999)] Windows NT 4.0 was the last released version. Prerelease builds of Windows 2000 for Alpha were distributed, up to at least Release Candidate 1 (build 2072), which expired 444 days after installation. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2009-January.mbox#L2396|"w2k alpha"]], Steven Moix, axp-list, 9 Jan 2009)] The last Windows NT firmware was AlphaBIOS V5.71, for the AlphaServer DS10, AlphaStation XP900, and AlphaServer DS20 and DS20E. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alpha_systems_firmware_update_v7.3_release_notes_overview.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Systems Firmware Update V7.3 Release Notes Overview}}, p. 10)]

==== Tru64 UNIX ====

Early versions were called DEC OSF/1, [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_1000:alphaserver_1000_owners_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 1000 Owner's Guide}}, p. 3-1)] and Digital UNIX was renamed Tru64 UNIX in 1999. [(>''Tru64 UNIX Operating System, Version 4.0F'', SPD 41.61.22, Compaq, April 1999)] The Tru64 UNIX V4.0F Software Product Description lists these supported systems: [(>''Tru64 UNIX Operating System, Version 4.0F'', SPD 41.61.22, Compaq, April 1999, Hardware Support Tables)]

  * DEC 2000, DEC 3000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000
  * AlphaServer 300, 400, 800, 1000, 1000A, 1200, 2000, 2100, 2100A, 4000, 4100, 8200, 8400, DS20, ES40, GS60, and GS140
  * AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, 400, 500, 600, and 600A, the Personal Workstation au series, and the Professional Workstation XP1000
  * the AXPpci 33, AXPvme and Alpha VME boards, and the single board computers EB66+, EB164, AlphaPC 64, AlphaPC 164, AlphaPC 164LX, and AlphaPC 164SX

The list omits some systems that V4.0F did support, such as the AlphaServer DS10, [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds10_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS10 V7.3 Console Firmware Release Notes}})] so the absence of a system from it does not show that the system was unsupported.

Tru64 UNIX V5.1 was the last version to support the systems without PCI, the DEC 2000, 3000, 4000, and 7000/10000. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L37862|"end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], William H. Magill, axp-list, 20 Sep 2000)] The AlphaServer 8200 and 8400 remained supported after V5.1, but without their XMI bus. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L39979|"Re: end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 22 Sep 2000)] The EV7 systems require V5.1B. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es47:alphaserver_es47_tower_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer ES47 Tower QuickSpecs}}, November 2003, p. 2)]

Minimum versions on the OEM motherboards were Digital UNIX 4.0 for the AlphaPC 164, [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164:pc164_digital_unix_product_brief.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164 DIGITAL UNIX Product Brief}}, p. 2)] 4.0D for the AlphaPC 164SX and 164LX, [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164sx:pc164sx_digital_unix_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164SX DIGITAL UNIX User's Manual}}, p. 7-29)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164lx:pc164lx_tru64_unix_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164LX Tru64 UNIX User's Manual}}, p. 7-29)] and Tru64 UNIX 4.0E for the AlphaPC 264DP. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc264dp:pc264dp_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 264DP User's Manual}}, p. 8-44)] The PC164 was sold in a Windows NT and Linux version and a Digital UNIX version that included a license. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L32037|"Re: Alpha-Boards"]], Stephen J. Gaudet, axp-list, 21 Oct 1996)]

==== Linux ====

Linux runs on most Alpha systems, booting from SRM with aboot or from ARC, AlphaBIOS, or ARCSBIOS with MILO. Systems without SRM, such as the Alpha XL and XLT and the Ruffian, can only boot Linux with MILO. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-September.mbox#L5923|"Alpha Core ISO Images"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 29 Sep 2004)] See [[software:boot_loaders|Boot Loaders]].

Linux 6.10 removed support for processors without the BWX extension (the EV4, EV45, and EV5 generations) and for the systems that can only use them. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/a4184174be36|"alpha: drop pre-EV56 support"]], Linux 6.10)] The removed systems are:

  * the Jensen (DEC 2000 AXP and DECpc 150 AXP), [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/f81f335a56a8|"alpha: remove DECpc AXP150 (Jensen) support"]], Linux 6.10)]
  * all systems with the LCA processors or the APECS chipset: the AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, and 400 and AlphaServer 300 and 400 (Avanti), the Alpha XL, the Multia and AXPpci 33 (Noname), the AlphaBook 1, the Platform 2000, the EB64+, EB66, EB66+, and AlphaPC 64, and the EV4 versions of the AlphaServer 1000 and 1000A, [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/430ad3f0ddd7|"alpha: remove LCA and APECS based machines"]], Linux 6.10)]
  * the EV4 and EV45 versions of the AlphaServer 2000 and 2100, and the AlphaServer 2100A (Lynx), [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/d2b1e353dacc|"alpha: sable: remove early machine support"]], Linux 6.10)]
  * the EB164, which only supports EV5 processors. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/4bf859076b16|"alpha: cabriolet: remove EV5 CPU support"]], Linux 6.10)]

Systems with EV5 processors that also support EV56 processors, such as the AlphaStation 500 and 600 and Personal Workstation, are still supported, but need an EV56 processor. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/a4184174be36|"alpha: drop pre-EV56 support"]], Linux 6.10)]

The kernel does not support the DEC 3000, DEC 4000, DEC 7000, or the AlphaServer 8200, 8400, GS60, and GS140 (TurboLaser): it has no machine vector for them, before or after Linux 6.10. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c]], Linux 6.10)]

Hewlett-Packard's Red Hat Linux 7.2 for Alpha was officially supported on the AlphaServer DS10, DS20, ES40, and ES45. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-June.mbox#L12427|"Re: RedHat 7.2 Linux for Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Jun 2002)] The DS25 and ES45 firmware release notes list Red Hat Linux 7.2 among the supported operating systems. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds25_v7.2_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer and AlphaStation DS25 V7.2 Firmware Release Notes}})] [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es45_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer and AlphaStation ES45 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, p. 6)] Installing it on the ES47, ES80, and AlphaServer 2100A required an HP installation wrapper CD. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L2911|"Installation HPWrapper ISO for ES47, ES80, 2100A and DAC960 systems for RH-7.2."]], George France, axp-list, 11 Jul 2003)]

The DS15, DS25, and ES45 (Titan), the GS80, GS160, and GS320 (Wildfire), and the ES47, ES80, and GS1280 (Marvel) need kernels built without ''CONFIG_ALPHA_LEGACY_START_ADDRESS''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2006-February.mbox#L7443|"Re: testing wanted: debian-installer, now with 2.6.15"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 9 Feb 2006)] Debian 3.1's prepackaged kernels did not support the Titan systems for this reason. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-November.mbox#L1252|"Sanity checking needed of Alpha subarch codenames in d-i manual"]], Steve Langasek, debian-alpha, 7 Nov 2004)] Linux support for the Marvel systems returned "after a long hiatus" in Alpha Core 3 in 2007. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2007-March.mbox#L1087|"Torrents galore...but has anyone actually tried it?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 7 Mar 2007)]

==== Systems Without Support for Every Operating System ====

=== No OpenVMS or Tru64 UNIX ===

  * **Alpha XL and XLT:** these have no SRM console. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L25578|"Re: Does DEC have a clue?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Jan 1998)] "OpenVMS will not run on an XL266. It would need an SRM console, and there isn't one for that model workstation." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-November.mbox#L791|"OpenVMS for Alpha"]], Jeff Donsbach, axp-list, 15 Nov 2004)]
  * **Digital Server 3300, 5300, and 7300:** these Windows NT servers "can switch the 33,53,73 to srm console, but they will still not boot unix or vms." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L13782|"RE: Compaq to offer Tru64 for $99"]], John Jemiolo, axp-list, 5 Nov 1999)]
  * **AlphaPC 164RX:** no SRM console was released for it. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L9150|"Re: switching from ARC back to SRM"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 16 Aug 2000)] It was only sold for Windows NT. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164rx:pc164rx_product_brief.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164RX Product Brief}}, p. 2)]
  * **AlphaPC 164UX and 164BX (Ruffian):** the only firmware is DeskStation's ARCSBIOS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L29483|"Re: AlphaBios vs SRM"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 15 Sep 1999)]
  * **Multia:** Jay Estabrook wrote in 1996, "VMS does NOT run on the UDB!" [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L1226|"Re: digital unix libs"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 1 Oct 1996)] and "Digital UNIX is not sold for the UDB." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-April.mbox#L47743|"Re: UDB OS questions, looking for OSF/1 media"]], Maurice W. Hilarius, axp-list, 27 Apr 1997)] Later, Tru64 UNIX ran under a hobbyist license, and OpenVMS with special firmware, but "Neither Tru64 nor VMS is officially supported on the box". OpenVMS does not support its serial ports. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L36|"Re: Multia/166MHz"]], Dan O'Reilly, axp-list, 1 Jan 2000)]
  * **UP1000:** Mike Foley of API: "Tru64 knows nothing about the Irongate chipset, nor is it a licensed platform." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L18082|"RE: UP1000 & SRM"]], Mike Foley, axp-list, 16 Feb 2000)] API's manuals for the UP1000, UP1100, and UP1500 only mention Linux. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1000:up1000_technical_reference_manual.pdf?linkonly|UP1000 Technical Reference Manual}})] [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1100:up1100_technical_reference_manual.pdf?linkonly|UP1100 Technical Reference Manual}})] [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1500:up1500_user_s_manual.pdf?linkonly|UP1500 User's Manual}}, p. 4-2)]
  * **UP2000:** "The UP2000 is not a officially supported platform for running Tru64." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L43541|"RE: UP2000 and Tru64?"]], Mike Foley, axp-list, 23 Dec 1999)] Tru64 UNIX 4.0F has nevertheless been run on it. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-June.mbox#L21700|"Re: OT: True64 on UP2000?"]], clc31@cornell.edu, axp-list, 28 Jun 2000)]
  * **CS20 and DS20L:** API NetWorks sold the CS20 for Linux; its onboard SCSI controller was not supported by Tru64 UNIX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L27576|"RE: DS20L"]], Peter Petrakis, axp-list, 30 May 2002)] Compaq's version, the DS20L, lists only Tru64 UNIX V5.1A in its firmware release notes. === No Linux ===

  * **DEC 3000, DEC 4000, and DEC 7000:** the kernel has no support for these systems. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c]], Linux 6.10)]
  * **AlphaServer 8200, 8400, GS60, and GS140 (TurboLaser):** the kernel has no support for these systems. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/kernel/setup.c]], Linux 6.10)] Jay Estabrook: "no Linux support for 8200/8400-class machines". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-September.mbox#L17415|"Re: AlphaServer GS140 (8400)"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 23 Sep 2003)] An attempt to boot Alpha Core 3 on a GS60 locked up. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-February.mbox#L341|"GS60 available for porting"]], Adrian Looker, axp-list, 18 Feb 2008)]
  * **Systems removed in Linux 6.10:** the Jensen, the EV4 and EV45 systems, and the EB164; see [[#linux|Linux]].

==== Conflicting Information ====

The following sources disagree with each other or with this page. They are listed here for further investigation.

  * **OpenVMS on the EB164**
    * {{wiki:hardware:motherboards:eb164:21164_motherboard_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|Digital Semiconductor 21164 Alpha Microprocessor Motherboard User's Manual}}, p. 3-10: "The EB164 does not support the OpenVMS operating system."
    * {{wiki:hardware:motherboards:common:srm_console_user_guide.pdf?linkonly|Alpha SRM Console for Alpha Microprocessor Motherboards User's Guide}}, p. vii, describes its SRM console for boards "running the DIGITAL UNIX and OpenVMS operating systems", including the EB164.
  * **OpenVMS on the Multia**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L1226|"Re: digital unix libs"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 1 Oct 1996: "VMS does NOT run on the UDB!"
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-June.mbox#L10065|"Re: Multia/UDB Comments"]], Mihaly HOMONNAI, axp-list, 20 Jun 1996: "it is possible to run OVMS on Multias."
  * **Tru64 UNIX on the UP2000**
    * {{wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1000:up1000_spec_sheet.pdf?linkonly|UP1000 Spec Sheet}}, January 2000, p. 2, lists "Tru64 UNIX" among the UP2000's supported operating systems.
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L43541|"RE: UP2000 and Tru64?"]], Mike Foley, axp-list, 23 Dec 1999: "The UP2000 is not a officially supported platform for running Tru64."
  * **Tru64 UNIX on the CS20**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L27001|"Re: DS20L"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 30 May 2002: "Tru64 ran on the CS20 from day 1, though it wouldn't use the onboard SCSI controller."
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L27072|"Re: DS20L"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 30 May 2002: the CS20 "could not run TRU64."
  * **Windows NT on the ES40**
    * ''FWREADME.TXT'' for the ES40 on the V7.3 firmware CD lists AlphaBIOS V5.71.
    * {{wiki:software:firmware:alpha_systems_firmware_update_v7.3_release_notes_overview.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Systems Firmware Update V7.3 Release Notes Overview}}, p. 10, lists no Windows NT firmware for the ES40.
  * **Windows NT on the XP900**
    * {{wiki:software:firmware:alpha_systems_firmware_update_v7.3_release_notes_overview.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Systems Firmware Update V7.3 Release Notes Overview}}, p. 10, lists AlphaBIOS V5.71 for the "DS10, AlphaStation XP900".
    * {{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_catalog.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation XP900}}, Systems and Options Catalog, September 1999: "available in a Desktop configuration with either Compaq's Tru64 UNIX, OpenVMS, or LINUX-ready (operating-system less)."
  * **Windows NT on the DS10**
    * {{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds10:alphaserver_ds10_brochure.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer DS10}}, June 1999, p. 6: "Operating Systems Tru64 UNIX 4.0F, OpenVMS 7.1-2, Windows NT, Linux"
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-February.mbox#L31980|"RE: UP2000"]], KirkE@paccessglobal.com, axp-list, 28 Feb 2001: "I don't think DS10/DS10L ever supported NT (at least publicly), I've never seen an AlphaBIOS for either of them."
  * **Tru64 UNIX on the AlphaServer 4000 and 4100**
    * {{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, p. 1-2, lists "Compaq Tru64 UNIX V5.2", a version that was not released.
  * **Last Tru64 UNIX release for older systems**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L37862|"end of life for DEC branded Alphas (and Tru64)"]], William H. Magill, axp-list, 20 Sep 2000, forwards Tom Blinn: "V5.1 is the last release that will support the older "DEC" branded Alpha systems (the ones that do NOT have PCI buses for I/O"
    * The same message summarizes this as "any EV56 or earlier processor will no longer be supported by Tru64 after 5.1".
  * **Linux on the AlphaServer GS80, GS160, and GS320 (Wildfire)**
    * [[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/sys_wildfire.c?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/kernel/sys_wildfire.c]], Linux 6.10, supports the Wildfire systems.
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L39933|"Re: 2.3.99-3 axp combo patch"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 29 Mar 2000: "It's not supported in current sources, but I know it's being worked on."
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-September.mbox#L13968|"Re: AlphaServer GS140 (8400)"]], George France, debian-alpha, 24 Sep 2003: "Although this hardware it is not officially supported by HP on the RH-7.2 distribution, it should just work."
  * **Latest Windows 2000 build for Alpha**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2009-January.mbox#L2396|"w2k alpha"]], Steven Moix, axp-list, 9 Jan 2009: "it's the most recent build as far as I know, build 2072"
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2009-January.mbox#L703|"w2k alpha"]], Alexander Huemer, axp-list, 7 Jan 2009, lists "professional build 2128".
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-June.mbox#L32828|"RE: Alpha win 2K/NT"]], Paul Bunn, axp-list, 26 Jun 2001: "Compaq dropped NT support for Alpha circa RC2 for Win2k."
