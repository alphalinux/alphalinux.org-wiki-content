  * August 2026: [[https://github.com/zherczeg/sljit/pull/364|Matt Turner's]] patches gave sljit a 64-bit Alpha JIT backend.
  * June 2026: [[https://github.com/capstone-engine/capstone/pull/2967|Matt Turner]] fixed several Alpha decoder bugs in Capstone and added test coverage.
