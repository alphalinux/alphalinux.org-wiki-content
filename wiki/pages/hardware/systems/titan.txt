===== AlphaServer DS15, DS25, ES45, and TS202c (Titan) =====

"DS15 (aka HyperBrick), DS25 (aka Granite), and ES45 (Privateer) are all TITAN-based machines". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-November.mbox#L1365|"Re: Sanity checking needed of Alpha subarch codenames in d-i manual"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 8 Nov 2004)] "DS15 CPU is indeed EV68, I think around 1GHz. DS15 core logic is TITAN, successor to TSUNAMI, and used in DS25/ES45. DS15 is intended as replacement/upgrade from DS10, necessitated primarily because of EOL on TSUNAMI chipset by INTEL." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L8451|"Re: Blimey New Alpha Workstation"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 2003)] In 2002 the ES45 was the only Alpha with 66 MHz PCI. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L7350|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 5 Apr 2002)] The TS202c (Falcon) firmware lists only Tru64 UNIX. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ts202c_v6.8b_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer TS202c V6.8B Firmware Release Notes and Update Procedures}}, September 2005, p. 6)] Each model was sold both as an AlphaServer and, with graphics, as an AlphaStation.

==== Specifications ====

=== AlphaServer and AlphaStation DS15A ===

[(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds15:alphaserver_ds15a_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer DS15A and HP AlphaStation DS15A QuickSpecs}}, May 2007, p. 2)]

  * One 1 GHz [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] with 2 MB of onboard ECC cache, in a 3U enclosure
  * 512 MB, 1 GB, or 2 GB of SDRAM, up to 4 GB
  * Four 64-bit PCI expansion slots on a riser card, two of them capable of 66 MHz, with no support for 5 V cards [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L8940|"Re: Blimey New Alpha Workstation"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 2003)]
  * Onboard Ultra160 SCSI controller and dual 10/100BaseT Ethernet
  * Internal or front access storage cage, up to 218.4 GB or 745.6 GB
  * Two serial ports, COM1 doubling as the remote management console (RMC) port, PS/2 keyboard and mouse, DVD/CD-RW, 400 W power supply

=== AlphaServer and AlphaStation DS25 ===

[(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds25:alphaserver_ds25_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer/AlphaStation DS25 QuickSpecs}}, May 2007, pp. 1-2)]

  * One or two 1 GHz [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264C]] CPUs with 8 MB of dual data rate L2 cache each
  * ECC protected memory bus and cache, 512 MB to 16 GB using 512 MB, 1 GB, 2 GB, and 4 GB options
  * Six 64-bit PCI slots on three PCI buses: slots 1 to 4 at 66 MHz and slots 5 and 6 at 33 MHz
  * Integrated dual Ultra3 SCSI adapter and dual Ethernet (10/100 and 10/100/1000)
  * Six hot swap disk bays, up to 872 GB, IDE DVD-ROM/CD-R combo drive
  * Two 500 W hot pluggable power supplies, with an optional third for N+1 redundancy

==== Linux ====

  * These systems need a kernel without ''CONFIG_ALPHA_LEGACY_START_ADDRESS'': "On the TITAN-based machines (DS15/DS25/ES45) the console (SRM) lives in that area, so the base address of the kernel needs to be AT LEAST 8MB and better 16MB." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-August.mbox#L1628|"Re: AlphaServer DS25 failure"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 6 Aug 2004)]
  * Peter Rival on the ES45 in 2001: "Works wonderfully. :) I use it all the time - just select "Titan" for your kernel type." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-October.mbox#L15863|"Re: Linux 2.4 on ES45?"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 18 Oct 2001)]
  * 2004 kernels crashed on the DS15 because "core_titan.c:titan_agp_info() code expects there to be two PA chips, and when there is only one (as in DS15), a machine check will occur". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-February.mbox#L3848|"Re: Dbian Linux (woody) for AXP can't boot on DS15"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 9 Feb 2004)] The "TITAN P-Chip (aka PA-chip) supports 2 PCI buses (or 1 PCI and 1 AGP), so the builtin devices (NIC/etc) are on 1 PCI bus, and the riser PCI slots are on the other". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L8940|"Re: Blimey New Alpha Workstation"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 2003)]
  * "both the SYMBIOS 1010 and the Adaptec 29160 and 39160 have been successfully used here on ES45 under Linux". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-February.mbox#L3437|"Re: U160 controllers for ES45/Alpha Linux????"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 5 Feb 2002)]
  * Debian's SMP kernels were broken on the ES45 and DS25 from 5.4 until about 6.0. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2022-February.mbox#L260|"MP kernels broken with version 5.4.0-1"]], Frank Scheiner, debian-alpha, 20 Feb 2022)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2022-November.mbox#L1|"Linux 6.0.7 MP kernel works on DS25"]], Frank Scheiner, debian-alpha, 11 Nov 2022)]

==== Power ====

A Debian build daemon, "an ES45 with three 1.25GHz CPUs and 4 Gbyte memory", drew about 600 W. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2012-April.mbox#L4811|"Re: Alpha ES45 buildd"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 30 Apr 2012)]

==== Conflicting Information ====

The following sources disagree with each other or with this page. They are listed here for further investigation.

    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2004-November.mbox#L1365|"Re: Sanity checking needed of Alpha subarch codenames in d-i manual"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 8 Nov 2004, includes the DS15.
