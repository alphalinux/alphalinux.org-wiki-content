===== AlphaServer GS60, GS60E, and GS140 =====

The AlphaServer GS60, GS60E, and GS140 are [[hardware:systems:turbolaser|AlphaServer 8200 and 8400]] systems with 21264 CPUs, "2-6 (GS60) or 2-14 (GS140) Alpha 21264 CPU processors". [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_gs140_product_profile.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60 and GS140 Series with Alpha 21264 Processor}}, Product Profile, Compaq, June 1999)] An AlphaServer 8200 could be upgraded to a GS60 and an 8400 to a GS140. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_8400:alphaserver_8200_8400_dec_7000_upgrades_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer 8200/8400 and VAX/DEC 7000 Upgrades}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, p. 2.211)] The three share one firmware kit, built for the E2063-DA and E2067-DA CPU modules, which "cannot be used with AlphServer 8200/8400 systems". [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_gs140_v6.6_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60/GS60E/GS140 V6.6 Firmware Update Procedures}}, p. 1-1)]

==== Specifications ====

Except where noted, the following comes from the product profile. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_gs140_product_profile.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60 and GS140 Series with Alpha 21264 Processor}}, Product Profile, Compaq, June 1999)]

  * Two to six [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] CPUs in the GS60 and two to 14 in the GS140, each with 4 MiB of ECC protected onboard cache; the GS60 was sold as the 6/525 model [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_8200_tru64_openvms_systems_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer GS60 and AlphaServer 8200 Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS Systems}}, Systems and Options Catalog, April 1999, p. 2.1)]
  * Up to 12 GiB of memory in the GS60 and 28 GiB in the GS140
  * Up to 132 PCI slots in the GS60 and 144 in the GS140, on an I/O architecture with multiple high-speed channels for PCI, EISA, or XMI
  * Integral UltraSCSI (40 MB/s) adapter for internal and external storage
  * 40 or 48 internal storage slots, up to 720 GB of internal storage in the GS60 and 864 GB in the GS140
  * Hot swap disks, RAID, N+1 redundant power supplies, ECC memory, heat, fan, and power supply sensors
  * Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS only; Windows NT was promised but never shipped

==== Upgrades ====

An 8200 or 8400 upgraded with EV6 CPU modules had to have its operating system moved to Digital UNIX V4.0E first, since V4.0D cannot run on the 21264. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L5713|"[SUMMARY]: Is 4.0e REQUIRED for EV6?"]], Mohan, alpha-osf-managers, 21 Dec 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L995|"SUMMARY: AS8200 -> GS60 upgrade:  Which comes first,"]], John Losey, tru64-unix-managers, 23 Mar 2000)] Upgrades used dual-CPU modules, such as one dual 500 MHz EV6 module replacing two EV56 625 MHz modules, and several owners reported doing it without changing the backplane. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-April.mbox#L10203|"Summary EV67 processors in a AS840"]], Gwen Pettigrew, tru64-unix-managers, 17 Apr 2000)]

==== GS60E restrictions ====

Compaq's release notes give the GS60E's KN7CG 6/525 CPU module (E2063) as needing Tru64 UNIX V4.0E, OpenVMS V7.1-2, and SRM console V5.5-12. The Ethernet connector on the KFE72 standard I/O module is not supported even though the console shows it; the KZPAA PCI SCSI adapter "has been qualified only for use with the CD-ROM device"; BA350 shelves are not supported; and "No EISA options are supported on GS60E systems." [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60e_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60E Release Notes}}, EK-GS60E-RN.A01, August 1999, p. 1)]

After a double-error halt (halt code 06) or a machine check in PAL mode (halt code 07) there may be no error log entry or memory dump; the console and PALcode instead save a halt frame in each CPU module's EEPROM, read with ''show eeprom halt''. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60e_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60E Release Notes}}, p. 2)]

==== Linux ====

Linux does not run on them. An 8400 upgraded to a GS140 loaded a kernel from aboot, but "It's HALT:ing right after it's trying to start the kernel." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-September.mbox#L12103|"Re: AlphaServer GS140 (8400)"]], Pasi Pirhonen, debian-alpha, 24 Sep 2003)] Alpha Core 3 on a GS60 "drew a bunch of gibberish on the screen and locked up". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-February.mbox#L341|"GS60 available for porting"]], Adrian Looker, axp-list, 18 Feb 2008)]
