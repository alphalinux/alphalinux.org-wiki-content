The AlphaServer GS60, GS60E, and GS140 are [[hardware:systems:turbolaser|AlphaServer 8200 and 8400]] systems with 21264 CPUs: two to six in the GS60 and two to 14 in the GS140. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_gs140_product_profile.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60 and GS140 Series with Alpha 21264 Processor}}, Product Profile, Compaq, June 1999)] An AlphaServer 8200 could be upgraded to a GS60 and an 8400 to a GS140. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_8400:alphaserver_8200_8400_dec_7000_upgrades_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer 8200/8400 and VAX/DEC 7000 Upgrades}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, p. 2.211)] The three share one firmware kit, built for the E2063-DA and E2067-DA CPU modules, which cannot be used on AlphaServer 8200 and 8400 systems. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_gs140_v6.6_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60/GS60E/GS140 V6.6 Firmware Update Procedures}}, p. 1-1)]

==== Specifications ====

Except where noted, the following comes from the product profile. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_gs140_product_profile.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer GS60 and GS140 Series with Alpha 21264 Processor}}, Product Profile, Compaq, June 1999)]

  * Two to six [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] CPUs in the GS60 and two to 14 in the GS140, each with 4 MiB of ECC protected onboard cache; the GS60 was sold as the 6/525 model [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs60:alphaserver_gs60_8200_tru64_openvms_systems_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer GS60 and AlphaServer 8200 Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS Systems}}, Systems and Options Catalog, April 1999, p. 2.1)]
  * Up to 12 GiB of memory in the GS60 and 28 GiB in the GS140
  * Up to 132 PCI slots in the GS60 and 144 in the GS140, on an I/O architecture with multiple high-speed channels for PCI, EISA, or XMI
  * Integral UltraSCSI (40 MB/s) adapter for internal and external storage
  * 40 or 48 internal storage slots, up to 720 GB of internal storage in the GS60 and 864 GB in the GS140
  * Hot swap disks, RAID, N+1 redundant power supplies, ECC memory, heat, fan, and power supply sensors
  * Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS only; Windows NT was promised but never shipped
  * CPU upgrades use dual-CPU modules; one dual 500 MHz EV6 module replaces two 625 MHz EV56 modules, without a change of backplane [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-April.mbox#L10203|"Summary EV67 processors in a AS840"]], Gwen Pettigrew, tru64-unix-managers, 17 Apr 2000)]

==== Linux ====

No Linux kernel has supported these systems; a kernel loaded by aboot halts as soon as it starts. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-September.mbox#L12103|"Re: AlphaServer GS140 (8400)"]], Pasi Pirhonen, debian-alpha, 24 Sep 2003)]
