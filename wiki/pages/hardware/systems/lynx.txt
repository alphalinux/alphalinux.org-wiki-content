===== AlphaServer 2100A (Lynx) =====


"The enclosure supports up to four CPUs ... Up to 2 gigabytes of high-capacity memory are supported (1 gigabyte on 4-CPU configurations)." It was sold as 4/275, 5/250, and 5/300 models, with "8 PCI slots, 3 EISA slots". [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_2100:alphaserver_2100a_owners_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 2100A Owner's Guide}}, pp. 1-2, 1-3)]

  * "Interrupt delivery/management is a bit different on 2100A (LYNX) vs 2100 (SABLE)". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L39379|"Re: AlphaServer 2100A compatability issue"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Sep 1999)] In 2001, "AS 2100A, aka LYNX, is NOT currently supported by Alpha Linux. Your best bet is one of the *BSDs". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-October.mbox#L7086|"Re: Problems Installing 7.1 on AlphaServer 2100A 5/300"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Oct 2001)]
  * HP's Red Hat 7.2 kernels added support in 2003: "We did slip in bits for the Lynx (AS2100A). :-)" [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-April.mbox#L2551|"Fwd: Re: workable alpha kernels"]], George France, axp-list, 2 Apr 2003)] Installing Red Hat 7.2 needed HP's wrapper CD. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L2911|"Installation HPWrapper ISO for ES47, ES80, 2100A and DAC960 systems for RH-7.2."]], George France, axp-list, 11 Jul 2003)]
  * Alpha Core 0.9 installed on an AlphaServer 2100A, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-October.mbox#L2929|"AC-0.9 Testing Results (was: Re: Alpha Core ISO Images)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Oct 2004)] but Alpha Core 1.0 did not: "Something is definitely broken WRT the SCSI drivers". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-May.mbox#L3648|"AS2100 and AlphaCore?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 13 May 2005)]
