===== Alpha XL =====


The Alpha XL 233 and 266 are Windows NT workstations with EV4 or EV45 CPUs. "The internal name for those boxes was "NT Dream Machine"". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L26311|"Re: Does DEC have a clue?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Jan 1998)] "The 233/266 versions, with EV4/EV45 CPUs will have *only* ARC console. There actually *was* an AlphaBIOS for the 233/266, but it never shipped". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L46358|"Re: DEC Celebris"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Nov 1996)] The EV5 Alpha XL 300, 366, and 433 are a different design (see [[hardware:systems:alcor|XLT]]).

  * "XL kernels and MILO will run on XL-233/266 as well as AVANTI", but not the reverse. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L48090|"Re: XL or Avanti?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Nov 1996)] With an Avanti kernel, "the floppy drive (on the XL) cannot DMA to all of CPU memory". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-December.mbox#L30289|"Re: Can't load RAMDISK on XL-266"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Dec 1997)]
  * With a Matrox Millennium, "you MUST be running an XL kernel, *NOT* an AVANTI one". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L34116|"Re: XL266: MILO/SCSI problem, X I/O problem"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 17 Jun 1998)]
