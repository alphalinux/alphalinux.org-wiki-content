===== AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, 400 and AlphaServer 300, 400 (Avanti) =====


Codenames: AlphaStation 200 Mustang, 205 LX3, 250 M3, 255 LX3+, 400 Avanti, and AlphaServer 300 Melmac. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L30004|"Re: AlphaServer 800"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Mar 1998)] The AlphaServer 300 has a 21064A with the 21072 (APECS) chipset, 2 MB of cache, and up to 512 MB of parity SIMMs. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_300:alphaserver_300_users_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 300 User's Guide}}, p. A-2)] These systems have both SRM and ARC.

  * The AlphaStation 200 is "identical to AVANTIs, as MILO and the kernel see them, although the MB is not daughtercard based". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-April.mbox#L9214|"Re: AlphaStation info"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Apr 1996)]
  * MILO must not be written to flash: "You MUST NOT, as the AVANTI family is not supported for doing this." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L59698|"Re: Questions."]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 1998)] Richard Henderson: "Booting and rebooting from SRM should be clean. That is how I boot my Avanti." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L16159|"Re: Kernel 2.1.131 compiles but hangs..."]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 7 Dec 1998)]
  * The built-in 21040 and 21041 Ethernet controllers on the AlphaStation 200, 250, 255, and AlphaServer 300 "give problems with the current default driver ("de2104x")". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2006-January.mbox#L4129|""no link beat found" on digital alpha-station 255"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 17 Jan 2006)]
  * The S3 Trio64 "is notorious, especially in AS200/AS400 boxes" (see [[hardware:other#pc_graphics_cards|Other Hardware]]).
