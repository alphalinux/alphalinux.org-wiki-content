===== AlphaServer DS20 and DS20E (Goldrush) =====

The AlphaServer DS20 (Goldrush) has one or two CPUs on daughtercards and two PCI buses, and runs the kernel's DP264 platform. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L24945|"FAQ info (was Re: Linux on Compaq XP1000)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Mar 1999)] The system is the Compaq product built from the same design as the [[hardware:motherboards:pc264dp|AlphaPC 264DP]], sold as a pedestal that also fits M-series cabinets. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20_windows_nt_catalog.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 Windows NT}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, p. 2.45)]

==== Specifications ====

Except where noted, the following comes from the Windows NT catalog. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20_windows_nt_catalog.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 Windows NT}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, pp. 2.45-2.47)]

  * One or two 500 MHz [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] CPUs with 4 MiB of level 2 cache, on daughtercards
  * 21272 (Tsunami) core logic with two PCI buses
  * Five 64-bit PCI slots, one 64-bit PCI/ISA combination slot, and 16 DIMM slots
  * Memory in kits of four industry-standard ECC DIMMs, up to four kits for 4 GiB; kits of 128 MiB, 256 MiB, 512 MiB, and 1 GiB
  * Onboard diskette controller, two serial ports, one parallel port, keyboard and mouse controller; SCSI and Ethernet are PCI options
  * Ten internal storage slots: floppy, CD-ROM, one further 5.25-inch removable media bay, and seven hot swap StorageWorks disks, up to 128 GB with 18.2 GB disks
  * Hot swap disks, redundant power supplies, ECC memory, heat, fan, and power supply sensors

The console and kernel identify Tsunami family machines by a variation name; a DS20E reports Catamaran: "Booting GENERIC on Tsunami variation Catamaran using machine vector DP264 from SRM". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-os-linux-alpha-mbox/googlegroups-2000-May.mbox#L16600|"linux does not find second cpu on DS20E"]], Wayne Hall, comp.os.linux.alpha, 15 May 2000)]

=== AlphaServer and AlphaStation DS20E ===

The DS20E is a separate design from the DS20, sold as a pedestal or a rackmount system. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, ER-K8F6W-UA.D01, pp. 1-3 to 1-7)]

  * Up to two [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] CPUs: 500 MHz 21264 (EV6) with 4 MiB of secondary cache or 667 MHz [[wp>Alpha_21264#Alpha_21264A|21264A]] (EV67) with 8 MiB [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, p. 1-11)], and later 833 MHz 21264B (EV68A) with 8 MiB [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/freebsd-alpha-mbox/2005-August.mbox#L42|"KZPCC-CE SCSI controller"]], José M. Fandiño, freebsd-alpha, 1 Aug 2005)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/openbsd-alpha-mbox/2018-February.mbox#L1|"OpenBSD 6.2 on DS20e panic: ffs_valloc: dup alloc"]], Cody Swanson, openbsd-alpha, 24 Feb 2018)]. Upgrade kits were KN310 for 500 MHz and KN311 for 667 MHz, in per-operating-system variants, of which the -BD suffix is the Linux one. Dual processors must match in speed and cache size [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, p. 5-10)]
  * Up to 16 200-pin DIMMs in four banks of four, populated in numerical order with four identical DIMMs per bank, up to 4 GiB [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, p. 5-7)]
  * Five 64-bit PCI slots and one slot shared between 64-bit PCI and 16-bit ISA; slot 1 takes half-length cards only [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, p. 5-11)]
  * A combination IDE CD-ROM and floppy drive, one half-height removable media bay, and a disk cage holding either four 1.6-inch or six 1-inch hot swap drives
  * Two 375 W power supplies with a bay for a third, sharing a single power cord; the system powers down as soon as fewer than two are running [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2001-November.mbox#L10237|"SUMMARY: AlphaServer DS20E, Power Supplies."]], Thomas Leitner, tru64-unix-managers, 17 Nov 2001)]; two serial ports, one parallel port, an Ethernet port, keyboard, mouse, and an unsupported USB port
  * The Linux boot log in the manual shows the rest of the onboard hardware: an SMC37c669 Super I/O, two DS21142/3 Tulip Ethernet controllers, and the ATAPI CD-ROM on ''ide0'' [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, pp. 4-21 to 4-23)]

==== SCSI ====

  * The original DP264 and DS20 motherboards have an onboard dual-channel Adaptec AIC-7895 SCSI controller, designed in for Windows NT systems, which is disabled because of a bug in the chip. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-May.mbox#L41852|"Re: DS20 Q"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 May 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/1999-July.mbox#L18583|"SUMMARY: DS20 Onboard SCSI"]], Robert Mulley, tru64-unix-managers, 9 Jul 1999)]
  * The DS20E had two disk backplanes, one with four Ultra2 drives and one with six Ultra3 drives. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L7150|"Re: Hardware question: AS1200, SRM, LSI 53c1010"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 12 Jul 2003)] The DS20E and DS25 have integrated Adaptec SCSI controllers, so installing on them avoids the ''qla1280'' firmware problem of the Debian installer. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2019-July.mbox#L1737|"Re: systemd woes continue"]], Frank Scheiner, debian-alpha, 17 Jul 2019)]

==== Linux ====

  * See [[hardware:systems#tsunami_systems|Tsunami Systems]] for notes shared with the other Tsunami machines.
  * Compaq documented Linux alongside Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS in the DS20E reference guide, which calls for SRM V5.6-3 or later to install Linux. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20e_reference_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20E / AlphaStation DS20E Reference Guide}}, pp. 4-20 to 4-23)]

==== Known issues ====

  * **Missing second CPU:** on some early DS20 CPU modules the second CPU could disappear from the configuration after a power cycle; ''show cpu'' shows whether both are present. Later module revisions fix the problem. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/1999-August.mbox#L11563|"Summary - DS20 missing processor.."]], Burch Seymour, tru64-unix-managers, 17 Aug 1999)]
  * **SRM ''test'' over a serial console:** on the DS20E the SRM ''test'' command crashed when run from a serial console until a keyboard was plugged in. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2002-August.mbox#L24715|"SUMMARY[2]: DS20E SRM test command crashing"]], Dejan Muhamedagic, tru64-unix-managers, 23 Aug 2002)]
  * **CPU daughtercards:** ES40 CPU daughtercards do not fit a DS20. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-October.mbox#L12881|"Re: DP264 Processor Modules"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Oct 2002)]
