===== AlphaServer DS20 and DS20E (Goldrush) =====


The DS20 has up to two CPUs, "up to 4 GB of memory", [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20_windows_nt_catalog.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 Windows NT}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, p. 2.45)] "Five 64-bit PCI slots and one 64-bit PCI/ISA combination slot", and "16 DIMM memory slots". [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds20:alphaserver_ds20_windows_nt_catalog.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 Windows NT}}, Systems and Options Catalog, June 1999, p. 2.46)] "DS20 == DP264 == GOLDRUSH (1 or 2 CPUs on daughtercards, 2 PCI buses)". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L24945|"FAQ info (was Re: Linux on Compaq XP1000)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Mar 1999)] See also [[hardware:motherboards:pc264dp|AlphaPC 264DP]].

  * "The original DP264/DS20 motherboards were designed with the Adaptec 7895 (?) dual-channel SCSI controller built into the motherboard. We discovered some problems with using it that has forced us to disable it." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-May.mbox#L41852|"Re: DS20 Q"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 May 1999)]
  * "There were two backplanes for the DS20E, one 4 x ULTRA-2, the other one 6 x ULTRA-3". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L7150|"Re: Hardware question: AS1200, SRM, LSI 53c1010"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 12 Jul 2003)] The DS20E and DS25 "have integrated Adaptec SCSI controllers", so their installation avoids the ''qla1280'' firmware problem of the Debian installer. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2019-July.mbox#L1737|"Re: systemd woes continue"]], Frank Scheiner, debian-alpha, 17 Jul 2019)]
  * "the ES40 CPU daughtercards will NOT fit into a DS20." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-October.mbox#L12881|"Re: DP264 Processor Modules"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 21 Oct 2002)]
