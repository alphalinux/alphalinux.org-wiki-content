===== Alpha Development Unit =====

<WRAP box right 340px>
^ Alpha Development Unit ^^
^ Host | DECstation 5000, over TURBOchannel |
</WRAP>

Before any Alpha system shipped, Digital's operating system work was done on Alpha Development Units (ADUs), prototype machines that were never sold. ULTRIX ran on the ADUs first, then OSF/1, whose development continued on them until the first DEC 3000 Model 500 (Flamingo) systems arrived. Support for the ADU was dropped before the first release of DEC OSF/1 for Alpha, although some engineers inside Digital kept theirs running through the first two releases. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-sys-dec-mbox/googlegroups-1994-December.mbox#L5386|"Re: Ultrix for Alpha (?)"]], Farrell Woods, comp.sys.dec, 21 Dec 1994)]

==== Specifications ====

No detailed specification of the ADU has been found. From Farrell Woods's description: [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-sys-dec-mbox/googlegroups-1994-December.mbox#L5386|"Re: Ultrix for Alpha (?)"]], Farrell Woods, comp.sys.dec, 21 Dec 1994)]

  * A washing-machine sized box holding the Alpha prototype hardware
  * A DECstation 5000 sitting on top as host and I/O processor and front end
  * The two communicated over TURBOchannel, with the ADU appearing to the DECstation much like a TURBOchannel option

==== History ====

ULTRIX on Alpha was an advanced development effort only. It ran on the ADU, was never carried to the production machines, and was never a product. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-sys-dec-mbox/googlegroups-1994-December.mbox#L5386|"Re: Ultrix for Alpha (?)"]], Farrell Woods, comp.sys.dec, 21 Dec 1994)] OpenVMS first booted on Manniquin, an EVAX emulator running on a VAX, before it ran on the ADU. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-sys-dec-mbox/googlegroups-2001-July.mbox#L7344|"Re: Compaq kills Alpha"]], Fred Kleinsorge, comp.sys.dec, 30 Jul 2001)]
