===== AlphaServer DS10, DS10L, and AlphaStation XP900 (Webbrick, Slate) =====

The DS10 is a single processor 21264 server in a 3U rack-ready or desktop enclosure, with Tsunami core logic and a single PCI bus. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds10:alphaserver_ds10_brochure.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer DS10}}, Compaq, June 1999, p. 6)] [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/sys_dp264.c?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/kernel/sys_dp264.c]], Linux 6.10)] The XP900 is "just a rebadged DS10 aimed at the OpenVMS workstation market". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-October.mbox#L981|"Re: Debian on XP900?"]], David Huggins-Daines, debian-alpha, 12 Oct 2000)] The DS10L is a 1U version: "CPU speed-wise, and memory expansion-wise, they should be identical", but the DS10 has "room for 4 HDDs (vs 1 on the 10L), 4 64-bit PCI slots (vs 1 on the 10L)." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-November.mbox#L3445|"Re: Seeking opinion on DS10 veersus DS10L"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 19 Nov 2003)]

==== Specifications ====

=== AlphaServer DS10 ===

[(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds10:alphaserver_ds10_brochure.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer DS10}}, Compaq, June 1999, p. 6)]

  * One 466 MHz [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] with 2 MiB of L2 cache
  * 21272 (Tsunami) core logic with 128-bit wide memory datapaths and a crossbar switch, 1.3 GB/s peak memory bandwidth
  * Up to 2 GiB of ECC 100 MHz SDRAM DIMMs, with ECC on memory and processor cache
  * Four PCI slots: three 64-bit and one 32-bit
  * IDE storage, or SCSI with an optional PCI adapter; the IDE controller is "part of ALI 1543C chip used in DS10 for multi-IO" [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-August.mbox#L52462|"Re: DS10's"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 31 Aug 1999)]
  * Dual embedded 10/100 Ethernet
  * Five drive bays (floppy, CD-ROM, and three open), 300 W power supply
  * Two serial ports, one parallel port, keyboard and mouse
  * Auto reboot, thermal management, remote system management, memory failover, error logging

=== AlphaStation XP900 ===

[(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_product_profile.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaStation XP900 Alpha 21264 Processor}}, Product Profile, Compaq, June 1999)]

  * One 466 MHz [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] with 2 MB of L2 cache, 128-bit wide paths, 1.2 GB/s peak bandwidth
  * Up to 2 GiB of ECC 100 MHz SDRAM DIMMs
  * Up to two available PCI expansion slots
  * Up to 20 GB of internal IDE storage, or 27 GB of SCSI with an optional adapter; CD-ROM plus room for three disks, or two disks and a tape drive
  * Dual embedded 10/100BaseT ports, 2D and 3D graphics support including multi-head, audio, remote console management
  * Sold for OpenVMS, with Tru64 UNIX, Linux, and Windows NT support planned for mid-1999

=== Console and PCI slots ===

The console reference presents AlphaBIOS as "the graphics interface for Windows NT and Linux", alongside SRM for Tru64 UNIX and OpenVMS. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_console_reference.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation XP900 Console Reference}}, EK-DS10V-CR.A01, title page)]

The PCI slots are numbered differently by the console and by the chassis: logical slots 14 to 17 are physical slots 0 to 3, counting from the bottom of the machine, and the bottom slot is the 32-bit one while the other three are 64-bit. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_console_reference.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation XP900 Console Reference}}, pp. 1-4 to 1-5, 2-11)] A power-up display shows the onboard devices: two DE500-BA Ethernet controllers on bus 0 slots 9 and 11, the Acer Labs M1543C IDE as ''dqa'' and ''dqb'' on slot 13, and graphics on slot 14. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_console_reference.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation XP900 Console Reference}}, p. 1-4)]

The release notes add several hardware notes: all IDE drives run at 16 MHz, and IDE drives do not support ID renaming, so clusters of DS10, ES40, or XP900 systems can hit naming conflicts. With SRM V5.4-2 the ELSA Gloria Synergy graphics adapter only works in the bottom two PCI slots, logical slots 14 and 15. The KZPCM-DA combination SCSI and Ethernet adapter has no connection for the disk activity LED, so the front panel LED stays dark when it is used. Digital recommended setting ''ew*0_mode'' to autonegotiate for 100 Mbit operation. Switching to the remote management console uses Ctrl/[ Ctrl/[ R M C, of which only the "MC" is echoed. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp900:alphastation_xp900_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation XP900 Release Notes}}, EK-DS10V-RN.A01, August 1999)]

The DS10L's IDE controller, in the ALi M1543C A1 southbridge, is Ultra-33 with 28-bit LBA. With a 250 GB disk, SRM V7.3-1 listed the drive correctly but Tru64 UNIX could use only the first 128 GiB, and list members concluded the limit was in the chip rather than the firmware. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/gmane-2008-May.mbox#L814|"SUMMARY: alpha DS10L and large IDE disk"]], Stoyan Angelov, tru64-unix-managers, 18 May 2008)]

==== Linux ====

  * "DS10 boots and runs LINUX just fine with IDE HD and CDROM." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-July.mbox#L19640|"Re: DS10 EIDE Disk"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Jul 1999)] See also [[hardware:systems#tsunami_systems|Tsunami Systems]].
  * "Tulip does have problems with the built-in chips on XP1000 and DS10". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L12052|"Re: DS-10 Networking problems"]], W Bauske, axp-list, 18 Aug 2000)]
  * The DS10 was one of the machines LinuxBIOS was ported to, at Los Alamos. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/milo-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L385|"[Milo-list] [Fwd: linuxbios on Compaq DS10 at LANL]"]], Jeff Garzik, milo-list, 30 Apr 2001)]
