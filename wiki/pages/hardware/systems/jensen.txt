===== DEC 2000 AXP and DECpc 150 AXP (Jensen) =====

"The Jensen was an EISA-based system with a 21064 Alpha processor running at 150MHz." The DECpc 150 AXP and DEC 2000 AXP "differed only in the bundled software." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L4787|"Jensen-HOWTO: First pass"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 15 Mar 1996)] The DEC 2000 Model 500 was code-named Culzean. [(>{{wiki:documentation:references:alpha_axp_system_reference_manual_v6.pdf?linkonly|Alpha AXP System Reference Manual}}, Version 6, 1994, p. D-2)] Olivetti sold a rebadged version as the R7-600. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L2952|"RE: How to complete a Jensen system..."]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Mar 1996)] The Jensen was the first machine to run Linux/Alpha, with a 32-bit kernel "on a JENSEN, followed by AVANTI (as400)". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2006-July.mbox#L572|"first Linux port"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 13 Jul 2006)]

==== Specifications ====

  * [[wp>Alpha_21064|21064]] at 150 MHz [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L4787|"Jensen-HOWTO: First pass"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 15 Mar 1996)]
  * EISA only: it is the only Alpha platform in the kernel without PCI [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/kernel/sys_jensen.c?h=v6.9|arch/alpha/kernel/sys_jensen.c]], Linux 6.9)]
  * No dense memory space, only sparse [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-May.mbox#L13068|"Re: Beta Xservers based on 3.1.2E for the Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 19 May 1996)]
  * The real-time clock is a PC part at a non-standard address: "The Jensen RTC is kind of strange - it's the same as a normal PC, except it's at the wrong address! (The whole Jensen IO subsystem is rather strange - DEC was just learning how to do it...)" [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-May.mbox#L6117|"Re: /dev/rtc on Jensen?"]], Linus Torvalds, axp-list, 8 May 1996)]
  * Both SRM and ARC firmware, but only one at a time: the ARC console can read the floppy drive and the SRM console cannot, and ARC cannot switch to the OSF PALcode that Linux needs [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L4787|"Jensen-HOWTO: First pass"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 15 Mar 1996)]
  * The Jensen and Noname "had only 64-bit mem bus, and thus required SIMMS in pairs, not quartets". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-December.mbox#L1027|"Alpha hardware"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 5 Dec 2008)]

==== Installing Linux ====

There is no MILO for the Jensen, because "Jensen is sufficiently different from the PCI-based systems that porting the MILO PALcode would be a significant undertaking", so Linux runs under the OSF PALcode of the SRM console. Since the SRM console cannot read the floppy drive, Jim Paradis's 1996 procedure partitions the disk and copies a kernel to it from the ARC console using the standalone ''minlabel.exe'' and ''copyboot.exe'' utilities, then switches the machine to the SRM console to boot. The SRM console also stores at most 8 characters of boot flags, or 16 on the command line, so his kernel understood ''INSTALL'' as an abbreviation for ''root=/dev/fd0 load_ramdisk=1''. Because SRM cannot read an fdisk-style partition table, Linux and Windows NT cannot share a disk. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L4787|"Jensen-HOWTO: First pass"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 15 Mar 1996)]

Digital later published the 1.7 firmware with an installation procedure, and early versions of aboot needed it: "You should be running firmware version 1.7". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L37622|"Re: Error received Installing RedHat AXP150"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 21 Nov 1996)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-February.mbox#L6582|"Jensen firmware 1.7 on ftp.digital.com"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 13 Feb 1998)] See Conflicting Information.

Red Hat 7.2 "has been tested on JENSEN and will boot and install directly from CD-ROM." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-August.mbox#L11016|"Re: Installing RH 7.1 on Jensen"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 14 Aug 2002)]

==== Linux ====

  * The Jensen needs its own kernel: "Jensens can't boot the generic kernel image, they need a custom one". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L8369|"Re: Debian on Alpha DEC 2000 AXP"]], Falk Hueffner, debian-alpha, 11 May 2002)]
  * Early glibc did not implement ''inb'' and ''outb'' for the Jensen or the EB164, which broke the graphics libraries. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L8456|"Re: EB164 libraries and hardware access"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Mar 1996)]
  * For X, "the only card supported on Jensen under XFree86 is an S3-based ISA card. The S3 driver had *extensive* hacking done to it to support Jensen". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-February.mbox#L13405|"Re: EISA video card for AlphaLinux Jensen XP 150"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Feb 2001)]
