===== AlphaStation XP1000 (Monet) =====

The XP1000 is a single processor 21264 workstation with Tsunami core logic, "MONET at 500MHz, aka BRISBANE @ 667". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)] It was designed by the same Workstation group as the [[hardware:systems:miata|Personal Workstation]] and resembles it closely, "both externally and internally, with the builtin Ethernet and sound, same style cabinet". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)] A Personal Workstation cannot be upgraded to an XP1000. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]

==== Specifications ====

  * One [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] at 500 MHz (Monet) or 667 MHz (Brisbane) [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]
  * 21272 (Tsunami) core logic, with two PCI buses [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/Kconfig?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/Kconfig]], Linux 6.10)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L30857|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 2001)]
  * "2 @ 64 and 3 @ 32 plus only a single ISA, therefore 1 shared/paired" slots [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]
  * Eight DIMM slots in two banks of four, taking registered ECC memory; a bank cannot be partly filled: "XP1000 has 2 banks of 4 DIMMs each; you'll have a dead box if you pull any out..." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L28888|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 22 Jul 2001)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2005-October.mbox#L5688|"Re: Description of error codes or tech manual for XP1000 wanted"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 11 Oct 2005)]
  * Cypress IDE controller, as on the Miata GL and DS20 [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-January.mbox#L289|"My lucky day"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Jan 2008)]
  * Onboard Tulip Ethernet and onboard sound [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L12052|"Re: DS-10 Networking problems"]], W Bauske, axp-list, 18 Aug 2000)]

==== Firmware ====

"there's no MILO for the XP1000." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L52620|"Re: Install RH 6.0 on XP1000?"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 29 Sep 1999)] "XP1000 SRM stopped with v5.9, while the others went on into the v7.x's." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2008-October.mbox#L382|"Re: SCSI controllers and SRM"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 1 Oct 2008)]

==== Slots ====

Put the graphics card in one of the top three slots: "Make ABSOLUTELY SURE the ELSA is NOT in either of the bottom two 32-bit slots, as those are on PCI bus 1". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L30857|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 2001)] See also [[hardware:systems#tsunami_systems|Tsunami Systems]].

==== Linux ====

  * The XP1000, DS20, and Miata GL share a Cypress IDE quirk, fixed in Jay Estabrook's 2008 kernels: "The problem on CYPRESS IDE machines (XP1000, DS20, MIATA) is fixed." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-January.mbox#L289|"My lucky day"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Jan 2008)]
  * "Tulip does have problems with the built-in chips on XP1000 and DS10". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L12052|"Re: DS-10 Networking problems"]], W Bauske, axp-list, 18 Aug 2000)]
  * Debian's generic kernel stopped booting on the XP1000 after 3.16, but the SMP kernel boots: "The SMP kernel also boots on my XP1000." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2017-April.mbox#L678|"Re: Debian kernel boot failure on Alpha [was Re: systemd FTBFS]"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 10 Apr 2017)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2018-December.mbox#L1051|"Re: Use SMP kernel for Alpha (udeb) builds"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 8 Dec 2018)]
