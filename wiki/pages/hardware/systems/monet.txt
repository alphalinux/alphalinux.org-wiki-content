The XP1000 is a single processor 21264 workstation with Tsunami core logic, code-named Monet at 500 MHz and Brisbane at 667 MHz. It was designed by the same workstation group as the [[hardware:systems:miata|Personal Workstation]] and closely resembles it inside and out, with the same style of cabinet and built-in Ethernet and sound, but a Personal Workstation cannot be upgraded to an XP1000. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]

==== Specifications ====

  * One [[wp>Alpha_21264|21264]] at 500 MHz (Monet) or [[wp>Alpha_21264#Alpha_21264A|21264A]] at 667 MHz (Brisbane) [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]
  * 21272 (Tsunami) core logic, with two PCI buses [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/Kconfig?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/Kconfig]], Linux 6.10)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L30857|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 2001)]
  * Two 64-bit and three 32-bit PCI slots and one ISA slot, which shares a position with a PCI slot [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)]
  * Eight DIMM slots in two banks of four, taking registered ECC memory; a partly filled bank prevents the system from starting [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L28888|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 22 Jul 2001)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2005-October.mbox#L5688|"Re: Description of error codes or tech manual for XP1000 wanted"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 11 Oct 2005)]
  * Cypress IDE controller, as on the Miata GL and DS20 [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-January.mbox#L289|"My lucky day"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Jan 2008)]
  * Onboard Tulip Ethernet and onboard sound [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L22318|"Re: Can someone check the specs of this machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Dec 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L12052|"Re: DS-10 Networking problems"]], W Bauske, axp-list, 18 Aug 2000)]

==== Firmware ====

There is no MILO for the XP1000. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L52620|"Re: Install RH 6.0 on XP1000?"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 29 Sep 1999)] The last XP1000 SRM release is V5.9-1, built in December 2000; [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L17376|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 16 Jul 2001)] other Tsunami systems received V7.x releases, and the XP1000 is absent from the V7.3 firmware CD. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2008-October.mbox#L382|"Re: SCSI controllers and SRM"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 1 Oct 2008)] [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alpha_systems_firmware_update_v7.3_release_notes_overview.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Systems Firmware Update V7.3 Release Notes Overview}}, p. 10)]

==== Slots ====

The graphics card must be in one of the top three slots; the bottom two 32-bit slots are on PCI bus 1. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L30857|"Re: XP1000-667MHz no boot"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 2001)]
