===== AlphaServer 1000A, AlphaServer 800, and AlphaStation 600A (Noritake) =====


"AS800 is known internally as CORELLE and comes only as 5/xxx. NORTIAKE is AS1000A, and can be 4/xxx or 5/xxx (PRIMO). Also part of the NORITAKE family is AS600A, known as ALCOR-PRIMO which is also 5/xxx only. All have EISA bus". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L14347|"Re: Alpha Server 1000A - Noritake Image (Kernel)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Jan 1999)] The Digital Server 3300 is an AlphaServer 800 (see [[hardware:systems#naming|Naming]]).

  * The AlphaServer 1000A has "Seven PCI expansion slots, and two EISA expansion slots" and up to 1 GB of memory. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_1000a:alphaserver_1000a_owners_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 1000A Owner's Guide}}, p. 1-4)] "Physical PCI slots 11, 12, and 13 (primary PCI) are located before the bridge. Physical PCI slots 1, 2, 3 and 4 (secondary PCI) are located behind the bridge. Some PCI options are restricted to the primary PCI bus". [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_1000a:alphaserver_1000a_service_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 1000A Service Guide}}, p. 5-34)]
  * The AlphaServer 800 has a 21164 at 300 to 500 MHz, memory "from 32 Mbytes to 2 Gbytes", [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_800_users_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 800 User's Guide}}, pp. 1-3, 1-4)] "Three 32-bit PCI slots, one shared 64-bit PCI/EISA slot, and two dedicated EISA slots", [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_800_users_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 800 User's Guide}}, p. 1-5)] and an onboard QLogic SCSI controller and S3 Trio64. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_800:alphaserver_800_service_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 800 Service Guide}}, p. 6-18)]
  * "Please, use SRM, as there's no MILO for any of that family." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L29808|"Re: AlphaServer 800"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Mar 1998)] "I don't believe the NORITAKEs have IDE ports". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-February.mbox#L7551|"Re: Debian on Digital 3305"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 12 Feb 2003)]
  * With 2.6.11 kernels, the AlphaServer 800 and 1000A could hang with a DE500-BA (21143) Ethernet card "if the network cable is connected. Simply disconnect the cable" during boot. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-September.mbox#L1248|"AS1000A"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 20 Sep 2005)]
