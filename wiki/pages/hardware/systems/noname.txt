===== Multia and AXPpci 33 (Noname) =====

The Multia, or UDB ("Universal Desktop Box"), is a small desktop with a 21066 or 21066A CPU, onboard TGA graphics, a 21040 Ethernet controller, and one PCI slot. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-February.mbox#L329|"BETA Xserver for TGA Available"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Feb 1996)] "DEC had an agreement with Microsoft that every "Multia" system would be shipped with Windows NT installed." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L4880|"Re: Unable to install redhat 5.0 on multia through serial port"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 7 Jan 1998)] The [[hardware:motherboards:axppci33|AXPpci 33]] is a Baby-AT motherboard: "The AXPpci33 (aka NoName), is based on the EB66 (includes the Multia UDB). This design was produced by Digital's Technical OEM (TOEM) group". [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/Kconfig?h=v6.9|arch/alpha/Kconfig]], Linux 6.9)] The two "use the same CPUs and have the same interrupt handling mechanisms", [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-September.mbox#L1751|"Re: interesting benchmarks"]], David A Rusling, axp-list, 3 Sep 1996)] and "we use the same identical MILO for both UDB and NONAME platforms." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L949|"Re: fmu on PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Jan 1998)]

==== Specifications ====

The following describes the Multia; for the motherboard see [[hardware:motherboards:axppci33|AXPpci 33]].

  * [[wp>Alpha_21064#Alpha_21066|21066]] or [[wp>Alpha_21064#Alpha_21066A|21066A]] at 166 or 233 MHz, with the memory and PCI controllers on the CPU die [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L4880|"Re: Unable to install redhat 5.0 on multia through serial port"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 7 Jan 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-April.mbox#L14880|"Re: UDB's"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 7 Apr 1997)]
  * 64-bit memory bus, so SIMMs go in pairs rather than quartets, and parity SIMMs are required; parity cannot be disabled [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-December.mbox#L1027|"Alpha hardware"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 5 Dec 2008)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-July.mbox#L9098|"Re: UDB RAM question"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jul 1996)]
  * Onboard TGA (21030) graphics and a 21040 Ethernet controller [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-February.mbox#L329|"BETA Xserver for TGA Available"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Feb 1996)]
  * Onboard SCSI, at IRQ 11, with an external SCSI port; riser cards came in several configurations [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-August.mbox#L9692|"Re: MSS sound and Avanti (was: MSS sound and UDB)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Aug 1996)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-April.mbox#L7305|"RE: Multia/UDB SCSI termination problems"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Apr 1996)]
  * PCMCIA slots, supported in Linux from 1996 with Jim Paradis's patches to ''pcmcia-cs'' and the kernel [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-April.mbox#L402|"Patches for PCMCIA on UDB"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 1 Apr 1996)]
  * One PCI slot [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-February.mbox#L329|"BETA Xserver for TGA Available"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Feb 1996)]
  * Flash holding SRM in two chips and ARC in the third [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L47543|"UDB flash and failsafe loader experiments"]], Eric Smith, axp-list, 29 Oct 1996)]

==== Performance ====

The 21066 "takes an EV4 processor core, removes half of the on-chip L1 cache, and adds a PCI interface on-chip. These two modifications tend to really bottleneck the memory access." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-April.mbox#L14880|"Re: UDB's"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 7 Apr 1997)] "A 166-MHz UDB performs about like a 90 or 100MHz Pentium. The 233-MHz UDB performs like a 120 or 133MHz Pentium." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L4880|"Re: Unable to install redhat 5.0 on multia through serial port"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 7 Jan 1998)]

==== Booting Linux ====

MILO was originally written for the Noname, "to run LINUX on them without requiring SRM console (and its license fee)". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L32014|"Re: Linux_is_Great!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Dec 1998)] MILO booted from SRM "defaults to 24Mb" of memory. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L64302|"Re: UDB only sees 24M of RAM"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Jul 1998)] For Alpha Core 2, use a text mode install "as the TGA in the Multia will prolly not work for GUI install". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2006-February.mbox#L3031|"Installing Alpha Core 2 on Multia"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 22 Feb 2006)]

==== Heat and Power ====

"The UDB's case is so campact that many folks have discovered that they have problems with their machines due to it overheating." [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-September.mbox#L10163|"Re: dec hardware"]], Vernard Martin, axp-list, 12 Sep 1997)] The 3.3V wires of its power connector discolor and fail. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-September.mbox#L7408|"UDB Power Problem Solved"]], Michael Brennen, axp-list, 10 Sep 1997)]

==== Linux ====

The ''de2104x'' driver "was known to fail on those machines (AS250/AS500/Multia) that have a builtin 21040 (old/original) TULIP"; use ''de4x5''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2006-February.mbox#L8047|"Re: testing wanted: debian-installer, now with 2.6.15"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 10 Feb 2006)]
