===== PC164RX =====

The AlphaPC 164RX is an ATX form factor motherboard produced by Compaq.

==== Specifications ====

  * 525 or 600 MHz 21164PC CPU [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164rx:pc164rx_product_brief.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164RX Motherboard Product Brief}})]
  * 1 MiB pipelined burst SRAM L2 cache
  * VLSI Technology Polaris system controller, memory bus at 75 MHz
  * 2 or 4 168-pin PC100 unbuffered SDRAM DIMMs, supports 64 MiB to 512 MiB
  * 5x 5V 32-bit 30 MHz PCI slots (one behind a PCI-to-PCI bridge, shared with the ISA slot)
  * 1x ISA slot (shared)
  * 10/100 Mb/s Ethernet
  * PCI IDE (PIO mode 4) with UltraDMA support
  * Floppy, 2x UARTs, parallel port, keyboard and mouse, real-time clock
  * 1 MiB flash ROM
  * Windows NT AlphaBIOS firmware
  * Standard ATX power supply
