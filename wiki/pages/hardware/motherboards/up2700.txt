The UP2700 was a motherboard that API NetWorks announced in August 2000 for release in Q1 2001, alongside the [[hardware:motherboards:up1500|UP1500]] and [[hardware:motherboards:up1700|UP1700]]. [(>[[https://web.archive.org/web/20010531143520/http://www.digit-life.com/articles/augustdigest/index.html|"August 2000 Hardware Digest"]], Andrew Yaschenko, Digit-Life)] Of the three, only the UP1500 was released, and Samsung sold it directly rather than through API NetWorks (see [[hardware:motherboards:up1500#history|UP1500]]); the UP2700 was never built. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-October.mbox#L35|"Does anyone know anything about the API/Samsung UP1700 and UP2700 motherboards?"]], Peter Petrakis, axp-list, 6 Oct 2008)]

==== Specifications ====

The UP2700 was to use the Caspian chipset and dual Slot B [[wp>Alpha_21264#Alpha_21264B|EV68]] processors exceeding 1 GHz. API also planned support for up to 8 GiB of 200 MHz DDR SDRAM, onboard SCSI, dual Ethernet, and 66 MHz 64-bit PCI. [(>[[https://www.theregister.com/off-prem/2000/08/27/alpha-strikes-back-against-intel/1158583|"Alpha strikes back against Intel"]], Mike Magee, The Register, 27 Aug 2000)]
