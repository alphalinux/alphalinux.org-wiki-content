===== UP2700 =====

The UP2700 was a motherboard slated to be released by API NetWorks in Q1 2001, along with the [[UP1500]] and [[UP1700]]. [(>[[https://web.archive.org/web/20010531143520/http://www.digit-life.com/articles/augustdigest/index.html|"August 2000 Hardware Digest"]], Andrew Yaschenko, Digit-Life)] Of the three, only the UP1500 was released.

==== Specifications ====

The UP2700 was to use the Caspian chipset and dual Slot B [[wp>Alpha_21264#Alpha_21264B|EV68]] processors exceeding 1 GHz. API also planned support for up to 8 GiB of 200 MHz DDR SDRAM, onboard SCSI, dual Ethernet, and 66 MHz 64-bit PCI. [(>[[https://www.theregister.com/off-prem/2000/08/27/alpha-strikes-back-against-intel/1158583|"Alpha strikes back against Intel"]], Mike Magee, The Register, 27 Aug 2000)]
