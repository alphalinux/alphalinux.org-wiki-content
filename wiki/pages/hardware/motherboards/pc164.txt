===== PC164 =====

The AlphaPC 164 is an ATX form factor motherboard produced by Digital Semiconductor.

<gallery right aligned nocrop>
wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164:pc164-1.jpg Digital AlphaPC 164
</gallery>

==== Specifications ====

  * 366, 400, 433, 466, or 500 MHz 21164 CPU [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164:pc164_product_brief.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164 Motherboard Product Brief}})]
  * 21172 core logic chipset
  * One bank of 4 or 8 36-bit 70 ns SIMMs, supports 16 MiB to 512 MiB of DRAM (128-bit or 256-bit data bus)
  * 1 MiB synchronous SRAM L3 cache
  * 4x 33 MHz PCI slots (2x 64-bit, 2x 32-bit)
  * 2x ISA slots
  * Intel 82378ZB PCI-to-ISA bridge
  * CMD PCI0646 IDE on PCI
  * SMC FDC37C935 Super I/O controller (floppy, 2x UARTs, parallel port, keyboard and mouse, time-of-year clock)
  * 1 MiB flash ROM
  * Windows NT ARC firmware
  * Standard ATX 3.3V power supply
  * 12.0 in x 9.6 in (30.48 cm x 24.38 cm)
