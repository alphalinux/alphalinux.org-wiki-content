===== UP1500 Compatibility =====

The tables below list add-on cards and memory tested with the [[UP1500]] under SRM 5.6-19. Only SRM compatibility is recorded: for storage controllers, whether SRM can boot from them. Whether a Linux or BSD driver works is a separate matter.

==== AGP video cards ====

Standard [[wp>Accelerated_Graphics_Port|AGP]] video cards are available with three different connector variants: 3.3V only, 1.5V only, and dual-voltage. The UP1500's AGP slot has the 3.3V notch, and thus requires that the AGP card accept 3.3V signalling.

Some video card BIOSes are also incompatible with the BIOS emulator in the UP1500's SRM.

==== Contributing ====

  * Only SRM compatibility is recorded, not whether the Linux or BSD driver works
  * Only cards that physically fit are listed; AGP cards without the 3.3V notch are omitted
  * A card's ID is obtained with ''lspci -nn''
  * Compatibility of hard drive controller cards (SCSI, RAID, IDE) is determined by the ability of SRM to boot from them

=== Identifying a card ===

In SRM:

  * Execute ''show config | more''
  * Search for the device entry that corresponds to the card, e.g.: ''Bus 02  Slot 05: 0525102B/217D102B''
  * Alphanumerics 1 to 4 are the device ID and 5 to 8 are the vendor ID of the card
  * e.g., ''0525102B/217D102B'' translates to ''102B:0525'' as the card's full ID
