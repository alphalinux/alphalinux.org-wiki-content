===== PC64 =====

The AlphaPC64 is a 33.0 cm x 22.1 cm motherboard released in 1995. Its code name is Cabriolet [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L6964|"Re: Weird Lockups II"]], Richard Henderson, debian-alpha, 20 Feb 2000)], and it was also sold as the Alpha PCI64-275 (part number EBP30-AN). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L4140|"Re: distributors, etc."]], Stephen J. Gaudet, axp-list, 4 Mar 1998)]

The AlphaPC64 was derived from the [[hardware:motherboards:eb64plus|EB64+]], and Linux treats it as a variant of that board, so the names Cabriolet, AlphaPC64, PCI64, and EB64+ are often used interchangeably. They are different boards, however: the AlphaPC64 is a slightly modified baby AT board whose mounting holes are offset from the standard positions. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L37647|"Re: Memory on my new Alpha PCI64-275"]], John Anstreicher, axp-list, 16 Dec 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L888|"Re: power supply w/3.3v connectors"]], John Anstreicher, axp-list, 30 Nov 1998)]

==== Specifications ====

  * Alpha 21064A CPU at 200, 233, or 275 MHz in a ZIF socket [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc64:pc64_motherboard_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC64 Motherboard User's Manual}})]
  * 512 KiB, 2 MiB, or 8 MiB external L2 cache on 2x cache SIMMs
  * DECchip 21072 chipset (21071-CA cache/memory controller, 4x 21071-BA data path, 21071-DA PCI bridge)
  * 8x 36-bit DRAM SIMMs in 2 banks, supports 16 MiB to 512 MiB with parity, 128-bit data path
  * 4x 32-bit 33 MHz PCI slots and 3x ISA slots (6 total, 1 slot shared between PCI and ISA)
  * Intel 82378ZB SIO PCI-to-ISA bridge
  * National 87312 Super I/O controller providing floppy, IDE, 2x serial ports, and parallel port
  * Intel 8242 PS/2 keyboard and mouse controller
  * Dallas Semiconductor DS1287 time-of-year clock
  * 1 MiB flash ROM containing Windows NT ARC firmware and debug monitor

==== Memory ====

The board takes 72-pin 36-bit true parity SIMMs, installed in banks of four. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L19972|"Re: questions about the Cabriolet"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L22939|"Re: Cabriolet"]], glenn murphy, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)]

==== Power Supply ====

The AlphaPC64 does not use a standard AT or ATX power supply. It requires an AT power supply with two additional 6-pin connectors (often labeled P9 to P12), each carrying three +3.3 V and three ground pins, in addition to the usual +5 V, +12 V, -5 V, and -12 V. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L21001|"RE: questions about the Cabriolet"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L22939|"Re: Cabriolet"]], glenn murphy, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)] On the board, the +3.3 V connectors are J27 and J28. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L18323|"Re: More Newbie crap!"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 9 Jan 1999)]

The keyboard and mouse connectors are placed together where an AT case normally has only the keyboard connector, so the opening in a standard AT case must be enlarged. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L21001|"RE: questions about the Cabriolet"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)]

==== Firmware ====

The flash ROM is large enough to hold both the ARC and SRM consoles at the same time, allowing Windows NT and Linux to be booted from ARC and Digital UNIX from SRM. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-May.mbox#L9521|"Re: Maxtor SCSI Drives and a UDB"]], Stephen J. Gaudet, axp-list, 7 May 1997)] To switch from ARC to SRM, select Digital UNIX from the "Machine Specific Setup" menu and power cycle; to switch back, set the SRM ''os_type'' variable to ''NT'' and power cycle. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L28390|"Re: FW: True64 on Cabriolet"]], Bob Palladino, axp-list, 12 Nov 1999)]

  * **ARC:** the last version is 4.49-7. It is updated with ''fwupdate.exe'' via "Install new firmware" in the firmware's Supplementary Menu. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-August.mbox#L10567|"help with firmware file"]], dca2, axp-list, 5 Aug 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1999-July.mbox#L4802|"Re: firmware upgrade?"]], Phil Oleson, debian-alpha, 23 Jul 1999)]
  * **SRM:** the last version is 4.8 (V4.8-1, dated 20 Feb 1997). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1999-July.mbox#L4802|"Re: firmware upgrade?"]], Phil Oleson, debian-alpha, 23 Jul 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-November.mbox#L28390|"Re: FW: True64 on Cabriolet"]], Bob Palladino, axp-list, 12 Nov 1999)] SRM was originally only available as part of DEC's evaluation board software development kit (part number QR-21B02-03) [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L25259|"RE: Cabriolet SRM firmware"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 15 Oct 1999)], but in December 1999 Compaq made the image freely available at ''ftp.digital.com/pub/Digital/Alpha/firmware/interim/pc64/''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L6991|"[eric@cirr.com: SRM firmware now available for AlphaPC64]"]], Greg Johnson, axp-list, 3 Dec 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L2208|"Re: Alpha Motherboards SDK"]], Robert Maynard Rhyu, axp-list, 3 Jan 2000)] In 2003, George France placed the most recent PC64 firmware files he could find (dated 18 Feb 2000) at ''ftp://alpha.crl.dec.com/pub/digital/Alpha/firmware/archive/pc64/''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-August.mbox#L7741|"Re: Firmware for AlphaPC64 aka EB64 aka Alpha PCI 64-275"]], George France, debian-alpha, 20 Aug 2003)]
  * **MILO** can be loaded from ARC with ''linload.exe'', or by renaming the MILO image to ''fwupdate.exe'' and using the ARC firmware update option. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-April.mbox#L6752|"RE: cabriolet and RedHAt 2.1 AXP"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 15 Apr 1996)] One user reported that flashing MILO overwrote the SRM console. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L15313|"Cabriolet SRM firmware"]], Ryan D. Schaefer, axp-list, 11 Oct 1999)]

Recovering from corrupted flash is difficult: unlike the UDB, there is no floppy-based fail-safe loader. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-November.mbox#L31744|"Re: FMU.GZ crashing"]], David Mosberger-Tang, axp-list, 17 Nov 1996)]

==== Linux ====

Linux supports the AlphaPC64 with the ''CONFIG_ALPHA_CABRIOLET'' kernel option [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/1997-July.mbox#L208|"Help requested compiling kernel: which arch is mine?"]], Terrence Brannon, debian-alpha, 6 Jul 1997)] and with generic kernels. Its interrupt handling code was reused for the EB66+, EB164, PC164, and LX164. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-February.mbox#L6964|"Re: Weird Lockups II"]], Richard Henderson, debian-alpha, 20 Feb 2000)] Because the Cabriolet SRM console may report the system as an EB64+, the kernel reads the interrupt summary register at boot to detect a Cabriolet and prints ''Detected Cabriolet: correcting HWRPB.'' [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-November.mbox#L5583|"Re: [PATCH] [RESENT] compilation warnings 4/6"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 8 Nov 2002)]

Both SRM (with aboot) and ARC (with MILO) can boot Linux. Jay Estabrook tested Red Hat 7.2 (2.4.9 kernels) on a Cabriolet using SRM and found it performed reasonably well. Booting these 2.4 kernels via MILO requires a MILO based on the 2.2 kernel sources. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-June.mbox#L12427|"Re: RedHat 7.2 Linux for Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Jun 2002)] The MILO image is named ''cabrio'' or ''cabriolet''; MILO images for other boards such as the UDB do not work, because of different I/O maps and interrupt routing. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L30004|"Re: AlphaServer 800"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Mar 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-April.mbox#L6738|"RE: DIGITAL PC64 with 64MB and Linux 2.1 REDHAT"]], David Rusling, axp-list, 15 Apr 1996)]

==== Known Issues ====

  * The board has no onboard SCSI, and the old ARC firmware can boot from only a few SCSI adapters. Maurice Hilarius reported that Intraserver 3140U-N and 4140UE-N cards worked, while the Diamond Fireport40, BusLogic BT958, and Adaptec 2940 did not. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L21001|"RE: questions about the Cabriolet"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 9 Dec 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L69662|"Re: XP1000 and Linux"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 23 Mar 1999)]
  * SRM 4.8 supports booting only from NCR 53C810 and QLogic ISP1020 SCSI adapters, according to the evaluation board SDK documentation; one user found that an NCR 53C875 adapter was not recognized. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L18434|"Re: (pc64-275) SRM v4.8 SCSI controllers"]], Robert Parnell, axp-list, 12 Jan 2000)]

==== Conflicting Information ====

The following sources disagree with each other or with this page. They are listed here for further investigation.

  * **EB64+ and PC64 as the same board**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L37352|"RE: Memory on my new Alpha PCI64-275"]], Bob Segrest, axp-list, 16 Dec 1998: "The names Cabrieolet, EB64+, EB64P, Alpha PCI64-275 and AlphaPC 64, all refer to the same ATX form factor motherboard."
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-December.mbox#L37647|"Re: Memory on my new Alpha PCI64-275"]], John Anstreicher, axp-list, 16 Dec 1998: "The PC64 motherboard (21A01-xx) is not ATX format. It is a slightly modified (thank you again, Digital) baby AT."
  * **Aspen Telluride**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-June.mbox#L10560|"DEC systems equivalents to Aspen Systems"]], Brian Cruikshank, axp-list, 21 Jun 1996: "Telluride PC64"
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-May.mbox#L8890|"setting the record straight"]], Randy Perry, axp-list, 12 May 1996: "Telluride 21064 7 PCI and 3 ISA"
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-May.mbox#L8946|"Re: linux on EB164+"]], Jim Paradis, axp-list, 12 May 1996: "Aspen Telluride.  This is an EB64+ with the PCI-PCI bridge."
