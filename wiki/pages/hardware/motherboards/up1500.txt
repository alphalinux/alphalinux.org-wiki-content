===== UP1500 =====

The UP1500 is a motherboard built by Samsung and released in 2001.

<gallery right aligned nocrop>
wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1500:up1500-1.jpg Samsung UP1500
</gallery>

==== Specifications ====

  * 800 MHz EV68AL with 2/4/8 MiB external L2 cache [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:up1500:up1500_user_s_manual.pdf?linkonly|UP1500 User Manual}})] (8.5 GiB/s [(>[[https://web.archive.org/web/20030311052727/http://www.harddata.com/Systems/up15brief|"HP-SPx264-1500 Hardware Features"]], Archived March 11, 2003)])
  * AMD-761 Chipset, ALi M1535D+ south bridge
  * 4x 184-pin DIMMs, supports 128 MiB to 4 GiB of ECC Registered PC2100 RAM (2.13 GiB/s [(>[[https://web.archive.org/web/20020606002508/http://samsungelectronics.com/semiconductors/alpha_cpu/up1500/up1500.htm|"Samsung Semiconductor: UP1500"]], Archived June 6, 2002)])
  * 1x AGP 4x (See limitations below)
  * 3x 33 MHz 32-bit PCI slots
  * DECchip 21143 Ethernet
  * ALi M5451 Sound

==== Limitations ====

  * The AGP slot accepts only 3.3V signalling cards, effectively limiting the selection of video cards to those supporting AGP 2x [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-November.mbox#L4291|"Re: Graphics card for UP1500, AGP Compatibility and Signalling Voltages"]], Alex Deucher, axp-list, 27 Nov 2008)]
  * Not all AGP cards that fit the slot work, see [[UP1500 Compatibility]].
  * The IDE controller does not support 48-bit LBA, so hard drive sizes are limited to 128 GiB (137GB)
  * IO space needed for PCI counts against the amount of memory usable by the AMD-761 [(>[[https://lore.kernel.org/all/20020913184310.B7498@jurassic.park.msu.ru/|"[patch 2.5.34] alpha: nautilus update"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, linux-kernel, 13 Sep 2002)]
  * According to Ivan Kokshaysky, the UP1000, UP1100, and UP1500's AGP is limited to PCI transfers: 
    <blockquote>There is quite fundamental conflict between the Alpha architecture and x86 AGP implementation - Alpha is entirely cache coherent by design, while x86 AGP is not (I mean native AGP DMA transactions, not a PCI over AGP). There are no such things as non-cacheable mappings or software support for cache flushing/invalidation on Alpha, so x86 AGP code won't work on Nautilus.</blockquote>

==== Memory ====

The UP1500 requires PC2100 ECC registered DDR SDRAM; unbuffered DIMMs do not work. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-February.mbox#L6661|"Re: up1500 specs sheets"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 12 Feb 2003)] Modules tested by vendors include Viking DDR64X72RPC2100 (512 MiB) and DDR128X72RPC2100 (1 GiB) [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L18275|"Re: UP1500 memory specs"]], Iwao Makino, axp-list, 18 Apr 2002)], as well as modules from Kingston, Kentron, and Corsair. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L18363|"Re: UP1500 memory specs"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 18 Apr 2002)] Iwao Makino reported better results with PC2700 ECC registered DIMMs under heavy use. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-February.mbox#L6366|"Re: up1500 specs sheets"]], Iwao Makino, axp-list, 13 Feb 2003)]

==== Firmware ====

The last SRM release for the UP1500 is 5.6-19. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-November.mbox#L4880|"Latest SRM for UP1500"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 27 Nov 2008)] The final SRM revisions were made by Samsung to fix issues Hard Data found while testing the UP1500, and were distributed from Hard Data's FTP site. Samsung's development team was then reassigned to other projects. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-January.mbox#L4822|"Re: UP1500?"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 8 Jan 2003)] The UP1500's SRM has a newer BIOS emulator than the UP1000 and UP1100, and handles more video cards. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-December.mbox#L2218|"Re: PCI Video Card?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 4 Dec 2003)]

==== Linux ====

Early Linux kernels did not work on the UP1500 without a patch; patched kernels were available from Hard Data. The onboard Ethernet and sound work, and an AGP Radeon 7500 works with XFree86 4.2 or later. A 400 W power supply is recommended. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-January.mbox#L4594|"Re: UP1500?"]], Falk Hueffner, axp-list, 8 Jan 2003)] Hyung Min Seo of Samsung sought to get UP1500 changes to the Nautilus code merged in 2001. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-August.mbox#L17232|"Alpha Kernel maintainer?"]], Hyung Min Seo, axp-list, 30 Aug 2001)]

==== Cooling ====

The original CPU fan is an 80x15 mm Nidec Beta SL (12 V, 0.09 A). One user reported that the fan controller is sensitive, and that many replacement fans did not work. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2009-December.mbox#L2256|"Alpha UP1500 CPU Fan"]], Will Givens, axp-list, 31 Dec 2009)]

==== History ====

Hard Data received its first UP1500 sample from Samsung in June 2001, with an 800 MHz CPU and 4 MiB of DDR cache. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-June.mbox#L2886|"Samsung UP1500"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 6 Jun 2001)] API NetWorks did not distribute the UP1500, so resellers such as Hard Data bought it directly from Samsung. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-August.mbox#L17570|"Re: UP1500"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 30 Aug 2001)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L12658|"RE: Bye-bye our API...Drove my Alpha to the..."]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 15 Jan 2002)] Samsung stopped making Alpha boards around early 2002, selling its last boards to vendors with a one year warranty. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-January.mbox#L4438|"Re: UP1500?"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 8 Jan 2003)] The 8 MiB DDR cache alone cost over $1,000, and Iwao Makino warned that UP1500s offered on eBay might not be production models. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-July.mbox#L5893|"Re: Sources for Alpha Motherboards"]], Iwao Makino, axp-list, 10 Jul 2002)]
