===== PC164SX =====

The AlphaPC 164SX is an ATX form factor motherboard released in 1997.

<gallery right aligned nocrop>
wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164sx:pc164sx-1.jpg Digital AlphaPC 164SX
</gallery>

==== Specifications ====

  * 533 MHz [[wp>Alpha_21164#Alpha_21164PC_(PCA56)|PCA56]] CPU
  * 1 MiB external L2 cache
  * 21174-CA chipset
  * 4x 168-pin 100 MHz unbuffered SDRAM DIMMs (see below)
  * 2x 33 MHz 64-bit PCI slots
  * 2x 33 MHz 32-bit PCI slots
  * 2x ISA slots
  * Cypress CY82C693U-NC EIDE controller and ISA bridge
  * SMSC FDC37C669 Super I/O controller
  * Floppy disk controller
  * PS/2 keyboard and mouse
  * 2x RS-232
  * 1x Parallel port

==== Memory ====

Both 64-bit and 72-bit (ECC) 100 MHz SDRAM modules are supported and must be installed in pairs. Modules up to 128 MiB are officially supported, but some 256 MiB modules work in reality. The following table lists some RAM modules known to work or not work with the 164SX board.

^ Vendor ^ Part # ^ Size ^ Chip # ^ Works ^
| Kingston | KVR133X64C3Q/256 | 256 MiB | D1608SV1R-75 | Yes |
| Unknown | 2910103282 | 256 MiB | S80016LK7TW-75AD | Yes |
| Unknown | Unknown | 128 MiB | 48LC8M8B4-8E | Yes |
| Kingston | KVR133X72C3/256 | 256 MiB | HYB39S256800DT-7 | No |
| Acer | 71.85325.111 | 256 MiB | HYB39S256800CT-7.5 | No |

Unlike the [[PC164LX]], the 164SX does not require ECC memory. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L33282|"PC164SX system building questions"]], Tony Hoover, axp-list, 21 April 1999)] ECC is enabled only if all installed DIMMs are ECC modules, and the DIMMs in each pair must be identical. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2001-October.mbox#L1028|"164SX Memory"]], Christopher C. Chimelis, debian-alpha, 10 October 2001)] Do not mix ECC and non-ECC modules. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L36682|"164SX memory"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 26 July 2001)] x72 modules are ECC; x64 modules are non-ECC. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L33387|"PC164SX system building questions"]], Brian Rerecich, axp-list, 21 April 1999)]

The 164SX uses the same core logic as the 164LX, so 1 GiB should work [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-October.mbox#L9324|"Memory for LX164"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 12 October 2001)], and users have reported running 4x 256 MiB (1 GiB). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-January.mbox#L10815|"RAM for 164SX"]], Marco Benton, axp-list, 23 January 2003)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-September.mbox#L6386|"Memory Upgrade for 164SX"]], Jiann-Ming Su, axp-list, 30 September 2004)]

Non-ECC modules tested and qualified by Digital (not a complete list of working modules) include the ATP AMU16V64E8S4G0, Kingston KTV164SX series (16 to 128 MiB), and Viking VE4641U4SN3-DC01, VE8641U4SN3-DC01, and VE16642U4SN3-DC01. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-December.mbox#L19586|"Which SDRAMs for PC164SX ?"]], Maurice W. Hilarius, axp-list, 13 December 1997)]

Single-sided Hyundai HY57V28820HCT-H 128 MiB DIMMs were reported not to work. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-August.mbox#L1885|"164SX memory"]], Iso-H, axp-list, 6 August 2001)]

==== CPU speed ====

The 164SX documentation lists only two CPU speed settings, 400 and 533 MHz; the other settings are reserved, and one user who tried them only got 400 MHz. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L4541|"164SX overclocking"]], Kurt Ludwig, axp-list, 5 October 1999)] No 600 MHz 164SX was ever made. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-November.mbox#L873|"searching for a wide alpha cpu heatsink"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 5 November 2008)]

==== Power supply ====

A 300 W supply is sufficient for a typical 164SX or 164LX workstation. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-July.mbox#L3987|"PC164LX board in an ATX case?"]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 4 July 2001)]

==== CPU fan ====

The PC164SX requires a CPU fan with a rotation sensor, and most standard PC fans cannot drive the sensor input directly. The connector also uses a different pin order: pin 1 is +12 V, pin 2 is the tachometer signal, and pin 3 is ground, where PC fans use ground, +12 V, and tachometer. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L4454|"Installing new cpu fan on Alpha 164LX Motherboard"]], Donald White, axp-list, 3 April 2002)] A miswired fan can damage the fan, motherboard, or power supply, and the board does not start without a correctly connected fan. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L3582|"Installing new cpu fan on Alpha 164LX Motherboard"]], Maurice Hilarius, axp-list, 3 April 2002)] Even when rewired, a PC fan on a 164SX ran but the CPU would not start, because the board did not accept its tachometer signal. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L1167|"Need 164SX CPU Fan"]], Donald White, axp-list, 6 January 2002)]

==== Firmware ====

The flash ROM holds only one console image at a time, either AlphaBIOS or SRM. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L37112|"Can't downgrade from SRM to AlphaBios on PC164SX ......"]], Thomas Leitner, axp-list, 23 April 1999)] The final releases are SRM V5.8 [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L25599|"SUMMARY: SRM compatible UW SCSI controller"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 30 January 2002)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-June.mbox#L1688|"Help needed for install on 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 June 2005)] and AlphaBIOS 5.7. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L5721|"SRM -> AlphaBios fails on PC164SX"]], KirkE, axp-list, 5 April 2001)]

To switch from AlphaBIOS to SRM, copy ''sx164srm.rom'' to a FAT-formatted floppy, choose Upgrade AlphaBIOS, then power the machine off and on. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/tru64-unix-managers-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L17373|"SUMMARY: Linux --> Tru64 on AlphaPC 164SX"]], Gernot Salzer, tru64-unix-managers, 13 Jan 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L54|"Upgrading PC164SX firmware"]], Rich Payne, debian-alpha, 10 April 2001)]

Around 1998, SRM for the Digital Semiconductor boards was licensed separately, through the Evaluation Board Software Design Kit (QR-21B02-03, US$75) [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-January.mbox#L55113|"SRM on the SX164"]], Kevin Wood, axp-list, 21 January 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-January.mbox#L3642|"SRM license issues"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 6 January 1998)], and the images on Digital's FTP site could only upgrade an existing SRM installation. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-September.mbox#L40627|"Need SRM on my 164SX MotherBoard."]], Rich Payne, axp-list, 22 September 1999)]

=== SRM V5.8 hardware support ===

  * SRM V5.8 on the 164LX and 164SX is the only pre-EV6 SRM that supports Adaptec SCSI controllers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L4657|"General questions about alphaserver 1200 (533AU2)"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 9 December 2002)] Support is limited to the 2940UW and 39160; other models (2940U, 2940U2W, 29160, 3950) are not supported by SRM, although they work under Linux. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-June.mbox#L1688|"Help needed for install on 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 June 2005)]
  * The console's BIOS emulator can initialize ATI Rage 128 and Radeon cards, which older Alpha consoles cannot. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-February.mbox#L20624|"ATI Radeon on PC164??"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 February 2002)] PCI Radeon cards were recommended for the 164SX. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-September.mbox#L3927|"Well supported PCI graphics card for 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 6 September 2002)]

==== Firmware recovery ====

On the 164SX, DIP switch 7 starts the fail-safe booter, which can update the console from a floppy. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L1|"Upgrading PC164SX firmware"]], Craig Small, debian-alpha, 11 April 2001)]

Switching from SRM back to AlphaBIOS on the 164LX and 164SX can fail. Running the AlphaBIOS update from SRM may hang when AlphaBIOS offers to initialize invalid NVRAM, leaving SRM in place. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L36300|"Can't downgrade from SRM to AlphaBios on PC164SX ......"]], Thomas Leitner, axp-list, 23 April 1999)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L64605|"AlphaPC 164SX, AlphaBIOS, and SRM"]], Eric Dittman, axp-list, 21 March 1999)] A reported workaround is to start AlphaBIOS from floppy (with ''arc'' at the SRM prompt, or with the fail-safe booter), then flash it using the upgrade option of the AlphaBIOS copy running in memory. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L5721|"SRM -> AlphaBios fails on PC164SX"]], KirkE, axp-list, 5 April 2001)] This may take several attempts. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L6199|"SRM -> AlphaBios fails on PC164SX"]], Donald White, axp-list, 5 April 2001)]

==== Beep codes ====

  * 6 beeps: checksum error when the image was read back from memory, reported on a 164SX with newly installed non-ECC DIMMs. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L28484|"164sx beep-codes?"]], Danilo Godec, axp-list, 17 February 1999)]

==== Linux ====

  * Support for the 164SX first appeared in Red Hat 5.0; before that, it needed its own MILO and kernel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-December.mbox#L3695|"anyone successfully instaled axp on 21164pc? (fwd)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 3 December 1997)]
  * The PCA56 CPU corresponds to GCC's ''-mcpu=pca56'' [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L39096|"different alpha cpu compatibility issues?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 28 March 2000)], and it was the first Alpha CPU to support the MVI instruction set extension. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L563|"Playing DVDs"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 3 January 2002)] The PYXIS (21174) core logic supports byte/word I/O, and consoles enable the BWX extension by default. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L18328|"ev56 optimization -> illegal instruction"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 14 March 1998)]
  * IDE: the Cypress chip supports multiword DMA mode 2, but without its driver the onboard IDE runs in PIO mode and heavy disk I/O slows the whole system to a crawl. Andreas Krebs wrote the DMA driver, which later became part of the kernel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-November.mbox#L1067|"IDE DMA driver for 164SX"]], Andreas Krebs, axp-list, 2 November 1998)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-January.mbox#L24323|"SX164 onboard IDE"]], Andreas Krebs, axp-list, 18 January 2000)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L16865|"IDE I/O slows down 164SX to a crawl"]], Andreas Krebs, axp-list, 12 October 1999)]
  * Cypress IDE DMA transfers can disrupt ISA devices (PCI devices are not affected). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L44075|"PCI devices causing machine lockups on SX164"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 31 March 2000)]
  * USB: the Cypress chip includes a USB controller, which appears in ''/proc/pci'', but its pins are not connected to the USB connector footprints on the board. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-February.mbox#L29728|"USB Ports on 164sx"]], Richard Henderson, axp-list, 18 February 1999)]
  * In 2016, Debian's generic 4.x kernels returned to the SRM prompt with "BOOT FAILURE" on the 164SX [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-February.mbox#L997|"can't boot kernel 4.x on SX164"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 20 February 2016)], while a kernel built for the SX164 system type booted. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-February.mbox#L2246|"can't boot kernel 4.x on SX164"]], Alex Winbow, debian-alpha, 25 February 2016)]

==== Conflicting information ====

The following sources conflict with each other or with the account given above. They are listed here for further investigation.

  * **ECC memory requirement**
    * [[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L33061|"Re: PC164SX system building questions"]], Eric Dittman, axp-list, 21 Apr 1999: "I tried a pair of non-ECC PC-100 memory on mine and they didn't work.  I installed a pair of ECC PC-100 memory and they worked."
    * Later reports cited in the Memory section say non-ECC memory works.
