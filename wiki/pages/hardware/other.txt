===== Other Hardware =====

Alpha systems used expansion cards and peripherals for graphics, storage, network, sound, USB, serial, and input. Digital sold many of these as options under its own part numbers, often rebadged from other vendors. Whether a card works on an Alpha depends on three things: whether the [[software:firmware|firmware]] can initialize it (and, for storage and network cards, boot from it), whether its Linux driver works on Alpha, and whether the card's addressing and bus requirements suit the system.

==== Common problems ====

=== PCI slot voltage, width, and speed ===

PCI cards are keyed for 5 V or 3.3 V signaling, or are universal cards that fit both kinds of slot. A card keyed for one voltage does not fit a slot for the other, and forcing it in can damage it. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es45:alphaserver_es45_service_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES45 Service Guide}}, p. 1-20)] The EV56 motherboards such as the AlphaPC 164LX have 5 V PCI connectors, and supply +3.3 V to cards that need it. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164lx:pc164lx_technical_reference_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164LX Motherboard Technical Reference Manual}}, p. 4-7)] Among the EV6 systems, the DS10 and ES40 accept both 3.3 V and 5 V cards, [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds10:alphaserver_ds10_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer DS10 Systems Technical Summary}}, p. 6)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es40:alphaserver_es40_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer ES40 Systems Technical Summary}}, p. 8)] while the DS15 accepts only 3.3 V cards. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds15:alphaserver_ds15_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer DS15 System Technical Summary}}, p. 6)] The DS25 and ES45 have 5 V slots at 33 MHz and 3.3 V slots at 66 MHz: a 3.3 V card works only in a 66 MHz slot, a universal card works in any slot, and a 33 MHz card in a 66 MHz slot slows the whole bus to 33 MHz. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es45:alphaserver_es45_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer ES45 QuickSpecs, Version 54}}, p. 5)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds25:alphaserver_ds25_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer/AlphaStation DS25 QuickSpecs, Version 21}}, p. 4)] All the slots of the ES47 tower use 3.3 V signaling, with PCI-X at up to 133 MHz and a 1.5 V AGP slot, [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es47:alphaserver_es47_tower_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer ES47 Tower and AlphaStation ES47 Tower QuickSpecs, Version 20}}, p. 8)] and the GS1280's standard I/O drawer has 5 V PCI slots alongside its PCI-X slots. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs1280:alphaserver_gs1280_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer GS1280 Systems QuickSpecs, Version 22}}, p. 14)] For the AGP slot of the UP1500, see [[hardware:motherboards:up1500#known_issues|UP1500]].

The DS10, ES40, and DS15 accept both 32-bit and 64-bit PCI cards, [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds10:alphaserver_ds10_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer DS10 Systems Technical Summary}}, p. 6)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es40:alphaserver_es40_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|Compaq AlphaServer ES40 Systems Technical Summary}}, p. 8)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_ds15:alphaserver_ds15_technical_summary.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer DS15 System Technical Summary}}, p. 6)] but the XP1000 guide says not to use 64-bit cards in 32-bit slots. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_xp1000:professional_workstation_xp1000_installation_and_setup_guide.pdf?linkonly|Compaq Professional Workstation XP1000 Installation and Setup Guide}}, p. 3-2)] The Personal Workstation's 64-bit slots have their own restrictions; see [[#personal_workstation_64-bit_slots|Personal Workstation 64-bit slots]].

No Alpha system has PCI Express slots: even the EV7 systems offer only PCI-X, PCI, and AGP. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_gs1280:alphaserver_gs1280_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer GS1280 Systems QuickSpecs, Version 22}}, p. 22)] PCI Express cards can be used only through PCIe to PCI bridge adapters, with mixed results. Under NetBSD, a USB 3 card on such an adapter works in a DS20L, but the adapter is not detected at all in a DS25. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/netbsd-port-alpha-mbox/gmane-2018-December.mbox#L1|"PCI on Alpha issues and DS20L usability"]], John Klos, port-alpha, 6 Dec 2018)] A Radeon HD 5000 series card with an on-board PCIe to PCI bridge works in a DS15 under Linux. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2020-August.mbox#L229|"DS15 with kernel 5.8.1"]], Ivan C. N., debian-alpha, 17 Aug 2020)]

=== x86 option ROMs ===

PC cards carry x86 code in their option ROMs, and Alpha firmware must run a graphics card's video BIOS in an x86 emulator to initialize it. When the console cannot initialize a card, the system can still be used with a [[documentation:srm#serial_console|serial console]], and the X server's own x86 emulator may be able to initialize the card once Linux is running. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2009-October.mbox#L5944|"Re: X11 server on alpha -- update"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 27 Oct 2009)]

Newer video BIOSes are often too much for older consoles. nVidia Riva cards could not be used as the primary card because of 32-bit instructions in their BIOS, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L34466|"Re: Diamond Viper550 PCI"]], Jeff Garzik, axp-list, 25 Jun 1999)] and no nVidia cards worked with the LX164 and SX164 consoles until nearly the last firmware releases. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-September.mbox#L4322|"Re: Well supported PCI graphics card for 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 6 Sep 2002)] The ATI Rage 128 and Radeon do not work with the BIOS emulators of most older systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L1474|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Apr 2002)]

SRM V5.6 for the AlphaServer ES40 added "an X86 bios emulator" to support the KZPCC-CE RAID controller. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 38)] On these systems ''show bios'' lists controllers with an expansion ROM, and ''run bios'' runs a card's ROM, for example the KZPCC-CE's configuration utility. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds20_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 5)] Some cards' settings, such as the Adaptec 7895's, could only be changed with DOS-style utilities run under AlphaBIOS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-May.mbox#L41463|"Re: Question about SCSI on DP264 motherboard"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 May 1999)]

=== Sparse and dense bus access ===

The core logic of older systems can only reach PCI and ISA memory through "sparse" address spaces. X servers that assume dense access, such as the nVidia server, do not work on systems that require sparse access, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-June.mbox#L12190|"Re: XFree support of Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 17 Jun 2001)] and neither do Matrox Millennium cards on EV5 systems with sparse access. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-October.mbox#L2929|"AC-0.9 Testing Results (was: Re: Alpha Core ISO Images)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Oct 2004)] Among systems that current kernels support, the [[hardware:chipsets#21172|21172]] (CIA-2) systems use sparse I/O addressing. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-March.mbox#L18328|"RE: Re: ev56 optimization -> illegal instruction"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 14 Mar 1998)]

=== DMA addressing ===

ISA bus-master cards do not work, because Alpha systems have "no PCI DMA windows in the address range 0 - 16Mb, which makes "true" ISA devices (24-bit addresses) unsupported". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L34907|"Re: Kernel questions (not mine)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Jun 1999)]

PCI cards that can only address part of the 32-bit space may fail. The SB Live's EMU10K1 "is incapable of issuing addresses larger than 512MB-1". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-July.mbox#L11087|"Re: SoundBlaster Live (emu10k1) on PWS433"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 18 Jul 2002)]

Network cards that only place receive buffers on 32-bit boundaries, such as those using the VIA Rhine, leave IP headers misaligned and cause floods of unaligned access traps unless the driver copies packets. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-December.mbox#L6642|"Re: Kernel unaligned accesses"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 11 Dec 2001)]

=== Personal Workstation 64-bit slots ===

The Miata (Personal Workstation a and au series) has a small table of cards allowed in its 64-bit PCI slots, which are in front of the PCI-PCI bridge. With an unrecognized card in one of these slots, SRM refuses to boot; the card can be moved to a 32-bit slot behind the bridge. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-September.mbox#L2942|"Re: 3d on alpha?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 4 Sep 2002)] The check can be disabled with ''set pci_device_override -1'', but the early Pyxis core logic has a scatter-gather DMA erratum (see [[hardware:chipsets#21174|Chipsets]]). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L3263|"Re: pci cards supported by SRM?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 9 Oct 2003)] A graphics card can run in a 64-bit slot with the override set, but cards that use DMA risk data corruption there, and the workaround mentioned in the boot messages is incomplete. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-os-linux-alpha-mbox/googlegroups-2003-March.mbox#L5674|"Re: Problems with X on a Personal Workstation 500a"]], Måns Rullgård, comp.os.linux.alpha, 14 Mar 2003)] Storage controllers in particular should not be installed in the 64-bit slots of a first-generation Personal Workstation. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2012-June.mbox#L1331|"Re: USB cards for PWS 433au"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 24 Jun 2012)]

Some notes on these:

  * **SATA disks cannot be booted by SRM.** [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2011-May.mbox#L4373|"Re: Recent experience with IDE/SATA PCI cards on alpha?"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 15 May 2011)] A common arrangement is to keep ''/boot'' on a disk that SRM can read, such as a small SCSI disk or a PATA flash module on the onboard IDE controller, and the root file system on SATA. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-February.mbox#L2136|"Re: Compatible IDE controllers (preferably for an ES45)"]], awinbow, debian-alpha, 25 Feb 2016)]
  * **AHCI** controllers are not enabled in Debian's Alpha kernel configuration, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2018-December.mbox#L1429|"Re: Use SMP kernel for Alpha (udeb) builds"]], Bob Tracy, debian-alpha, 8 Dec 2018)] and no reports of them working on Alpha are known.
  * **Radeon.** Since Linux 6.3, ''radeon'' can be loaded as a module; [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/b6b17a8b3ecd|"alpha: fix R_ALPHA_LITERAL reloc for large modules"]], Linux 6.3)] from about Linux 3.11 until then, the module was too large to load on Alpha, failing with "Relocation (type 4) overflow", and had to be built into the kernel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2013-August.mbox#L808|"Re: new radeon firmware for 3.11.0-rcX"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 25 Aug 2013)] The driver needs current firmware from ''linux-firmware''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2013-August.mbox#L713|"new radeon firmware for 3.11.0-rcX"]], Bob Tracy, debian-alpha, 22 Aug 2013)] Linux 5.11 introduced a hard lockup with a Radeon HD 4350 in an XP1000, bisected to the driver's new memory allocator; no fix has been reported. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2021-April.mbox#L137|"Re: X11 system lockup with 5.11.0 kernel"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 5 Apr 2021)]
  * **USB 3.** No reports of xHCI cards under Linux on Alpha are known. Under NetBSD, a USB 3 card works on the DS20L, even through a PCIe to PCI adapter. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/netbsd-port-alpha-mbox/gmane-2018-December.mbox#L1|"PCI on Alpha issues and DS20L usability"]], John Klos, port-alpha, 6 Dec 2018)]
  * **Intel gigabit cards:** the PCI and PCI-X cards use the ''e1000'' driver and work well. ''e1000e'' is the driver for Intel's PCI Express cards, which need a PCIe slot that no Alpha has. Likewise, the newer LSI SAS cards (''mpt2sas'', ''mpt3sas'') are PCI Express cards.

=== TGA and TGA2 ===

The ZLXp-E cards use Digital's DC21030 "TGA" chip. The ZLXp-E1 is 8-plane, the E2 24-plane, and the E3 24-plane with a Z-buffer and 16 MiB. The Multia has the equivalent of an E1 built into the motherboard. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L44573|"Re: TGA graphics stuff (feature question)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 1996)] The kernel's ''tgafb'' driver supports all three. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst?h=v6.10|Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst]], Linux 6.10)] The ZLXp-L cards use a different accelerator chipset and are not supported. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-November.mbox#L21028|"Re: ZLXp-L Graphics Cards"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Nov 1998)]

The TGA2 cards, the PowerStorm 3D30 and 4D20, use a Cirrus VGA chip for the text-mode console and the TGA2 chip for accelerated X. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L18272|"Re: Graphics in my PWS433au Miata"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jan 2002)] The kernel framebuffer driver does not support them, but they can be used with the standard VGA text console driver. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst?h=v6.10|Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst]], Linux 6.10)] The PBXGB-AA's switch should be set to position six (1024x768 at 72Hz). [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)] The PowerStorm 350 has no X driver. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L10236|"Re: 7.2 iso's and Powerstorm video card"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Dec 2002)]

=== PC graphics cards ===

In reports from 2001 to 2008, cards that worked well with the X servers of the time included:

  * the ELSA GLoria Synergy, which passed both console and X tests on an AlphaServer 1200, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L5533|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 4 Apr 2002)]
  * Permedia 3 cards such as the Oxygen VX1, which have 32 MiB of memory and a fast RAMDAC, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L1474|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Apr 2002)]
  * the Matrox Millennium I and II, G200, and G400, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L4242|"fedora x install"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 28 Apr 2008)]
  * the 3dfx Voodoo3 3000, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-April.mbox#L4378|"Another test iso image from AC1.0"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Apr 2005)]
  * ATI Radeons, which are the best choice for EV6 and later systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L4437|"fedora x install"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 28 Apr 2008)]

S3 Trio64 cards are unreliable, particularly in the AlphaStation 200 and 400, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L17110|"Re: more pain in the arse"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Mar 2000)] and the Trio64V+ works poorly. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L2797|"Re: XF86 for S3 Trio 64 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Jul 2003)] On the UP1000, UP1100, and UP1500, the AGP slot operates only as a PCI slot; see [[hardware:motherboards:up1000#known_issues|UP1000]].

The ''radeon'' KMS driver is the only X.Org driver known to work on Alpha today; the older X drivers for cards such as the Permedia 2 and S3 Trio64V2 have been reduced to basic 2D support or removed. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-April.mbox#L636|"Re: Multia state of support"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 3 Apr 2016)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2021-October.mbox#L532|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] Alpha gfx card support for Xorg"]], Jason Howe, gentoo-alpha, 15 Oct 2021)] The ''radeon'' X driver requires the kernel's mode setting, since its user mode setting code was removed. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2014-December.mbox#L310|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] Re: radeon questions"]], Alex Deucher, gentoo-alpha, 11 Dec 2014)] In 2021 a PCI Radeon HD 3450 ran a 1920x1200 display from a DS20. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2021-October.mbox#L532|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] Alpha gfx card support for Xorg"]], Jason Howe, gentoo-alpha, 15 Oct 2021)]

=== Console restrictions ===

  * On the AlphaServer 4000 and 4100, "VGAs configured as console display devices must be in PCI0." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-5)]
  * On the ES40, "Only one VGA graphic controller card, that is VGA-Enabled, is supported in PCI0 bus slots." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)]
  * With SRM V5.9 and earlier on the ES40, an Adaptec controller in a lower-numbered slot than an Oxygen VX1 "may cause a console hang". [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 19)]
  * On the UX164 (Ruffian), the VGA card must be in the single 64-bit slot, because it is in front of the built-in PCI-PCI bridge. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L26949|"Re: Installing Linux on AlphaPC 164UX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 12 Jun 1998)]
  * The AlphaServer 800's onboard S3 Trio64 must be disabled with jumper J27 to use another VGA card. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_800_users_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 800 User's Guide}}, November 1997, p. 11-9)]

=== Booting from SRM ===

The AlphaServer 4000 and 4100 SRM console can boot from the KFPSA, KZPDA, KZPSA, KZPSC, KZPAC, KZPBA, KZPAA, and CIPCA storage adapters, the DE435, DE450, DE500, and DEFPA network adapters, the floppy drive, and the CD-ROM. It "does not support any EISA options as boot devices." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-4)] The SRM console for evaluation boards and OEM motherboards has drivers for the NCR 810 and QLogic ISP1020 SCSI controllers, and the DC21040 and DE205 Ethernet controllers. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:common:srm_console_user_guide.pdf?linkonly|Alpha SRM Console for Alpha Microprocessor Motherboards User's Guide}}, September 1997, p. 1-3)]

For third-party cards, the SRM console supports QLogic ISP1020 and ISP1040 SCSI controllers and most NCR/Symbios controllers except the 885. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-July.mbox#L505|"Re: PWS433a and tru64"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 4 Jul 2000)] The consoles do not support BusLogic cards. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-June.mbox#L15694|"Re: Any1 with a working PC164!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 13 Jun 1997)] Later SRM releases added some Adaptec controllers, the Intel EEPro100, the NCR/Symbios 896, and some Mylex RAID controllers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L1114|"Re: srm and scsi controllers."]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 6 Sep 2000)] SRM can boot from Adaptec cards on the AlphaPC 164LX and 164SX with firmware 5.8, and on the DS10, DS20, ES40, and XP1000 with 5.9. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L35378|"Re: Alpha PC164, SRM, and Adaptec 2940UW"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Apr 2001)] Some DVD drives are incompatible with the SRM console, usually because of their block size. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L12579|"Re: would like to do more upgrades"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Dec 2002)]

The Linux driver required firmware 2.73 or later, but many of the older DAC960s sold by Digital have Digital's custom firmware 2.70 or older. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-February.mbox#L19351|"DAC960 Alpha support (was: Re: Redhat 7.2 on a DEC ALPHA 2100)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Feb 2003)]
