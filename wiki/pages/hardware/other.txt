===== Other Hardware =====

Alpha systems used expansion cards and peripherals for graphics, storage, network, sound, USB, serial, and input. Digital sold many of these as options under its own part numbers, often rebadged from other vendors. Whether a card works on an Alpha depends on three things: whether the [[software:firmware|firmware]] can initialize it (and, for storage and network cards, boot from it), whether its Linux driver works on Alpha, and whether the card's addressing and bus requirements suit the system.

==== Common problems ====

=== x86 option ROMs ===

PC cards carry x86 code in their option ROMs. Alpha firmware must run a graphics card's video BIOS in an x86 emulator to initialize it. The emulator in MILO was not robust, and cards such as the ATI Mach32 and Mach64 depend on 386 and 486 instructions. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-January.mbox#L3377|"Re: interesting MILO problem w/card detection"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 16 Jan 1996)] MILO could be built with Digital's own BIOS emulation or with a free alternative. [(>{{wiki:software:boot_loaders:milo_howto.pdf?linkonly|Alpha Miniloader Howto}}, v0.85, 15 April 1997, pp. 4-5)] The Jensen's SRM console had no BIOS emulator. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-March.mbox#L36166|"Re: S3 928 video card on Jensen"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Mar 2001)]

Newer video BIOSes were often too much for older consoles. nVidia Riva cards could not be used as the primary card because of 32-bit instructions in their BIOS, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L34466|"Re: Diamond Viper550 PCI"]], Jeff Garzik, axp-list, 25 Jun 1999)] and no nVidia cards worked with the LX164 and SX164 consoles until nearly the last firmware releases. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-September.mbox#L4322|"Re: Well supported PCI graphics card for 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 6 Sep 2002)] The ATI Rage 128 and Radeon do not work with the BIOS emulators of most older systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L1474|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Apr 2002)] The X server's own x86 emulator can initialize video cards that the SRM console fails to initialize. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2009-October.mbox#L5944|"Re: X11 server on alpha -- update"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 27 Oct 2009)]

SRM V5.6 for the AlphaServer ES40 added "an X86 bios emulator" to support the KZPCC-CE RAID controller. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 38)] On these systems ''show bios'' lists controllers with an expansion ROM, and ''run bios'' runs a card's ROM, for example the KZPCC-CE's configuration utility. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_ds20_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer DS20 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 5)] Some cards' settings, such as the Adaptec 7895's, could only be changed with DOS-style utilities run under AlphaBIOS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-May.mbox#L41463|"Re: Question about SCSI on DP264 motherboard"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 May 1999)]

=== Sparse and dense bus access ===

The core logic of older systems can only reach PCI and ISA memory through "sparse" address spaces. The Jensen has no dense access to bus memory at all, and sparse accesses cannot be made transparently. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-April.mbox#L36611|"Re: Ati mach64 & alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Apr 1999)] X servers that assume dense access, such as the nVidia server, do not work on systems that require sparse access, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-June.mbox#L12190|"Re: XFree support of Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 17 Jun 2001)] and neither do Matrox Millennium cards on EV4 and EV5 systems with sparse access. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-October.mbox#L2929|"AC-0.9 Testing Results (was: Re: Alpha Core ISO Images)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Oct 2004)]

=== DMA addressing ===

Alpha systems have "no PCI DMA windows in the address range 0 - 16Mb, which makes "true" ISA devices (24-bit addresses) unsupported", including ISA bus masters such as the Adaptec 1540. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-June.mbox#L34907|"Re: Kernel questions (not mine)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 25 Jun 1999)] The kernel limits ISA DMA to 16MB on the Ruffian and to 2GB on the Sable and some Alcors. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/arch/alpha/include/asm/dma.h?h=v6.10|arch/alpha/include/asm/dma.h]], Linux 6.10)]

PCI cards that can only address part of the 32-bit space may fail. The SB Live's EMU10K1 "is incapable of issuing addresses larger than 512MB-1". [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-July.mbox#L11087|"Re: SoundBlaster Live (emu10k1) on PWS433"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 18 Jul 2002)]

Network cards that only place receive buffers on 32-bit boundaries, such as those using the VIA Rhine, leave IP headers misaligned and cause floods of unaligned access traps unless the driver copies packets. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-December.mbox#L6642|"Re: Kernel unaligned accesses"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 11 Dec 2001)]

=== Personal Workstation 64-bit slots ===

The Miata (Personal Workstation a and au series) has a small table of cards allowed in its 64-bit PCI slots, which are in front of the PCI-PCI bridge. With an unrecognized card in one of these slots, SRM refuses to boot; the card can be moved to a 32-bit slot behind the bridge. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-September.mbox#L2942|"Re: 3d on alpha?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 4 Sep 2002)] The check can be disabled with ''set pci_device_override -1'', but the early Pyxis core logic has a scatter-gather DMA erratum (see [[hardware:chipsets#21174|Chipsets]]). [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2003-October.mbox#L3263|"Re: pci cards supported by SRM?"]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 9 Oct 2003)] A graphics card can run in a 64-bit slot with the override set, but cards that use DMA risk data corruption there, and the workaround mentioned in the boot messages is incomplete. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/comp-os-linux-alpha-mbox/googlegroups-2003-March.mbox#L5674|"Re: Problems with X on a Personal Workstation 500a"]], Måns Rullgård, comp.os.linux.alpha, 14 Mar 2003)] Storage controllers in particular should not be installed in the 64-bit slots of a first-generation Personal Workstation. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2012-June.mbox#L1331|"Re: USB cards for PWS 433au"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 24 Jun 2012)]

Some notes on these:

  * **SATA disks cannot be booted by SRM.** [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2011-May.mbox#L4373|"Re: Recent experience with IDE/SATA PCI cards on alpha?"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 15 May 2011)] A common arrangement is to keep ''/boot'' on a disk that SRM can read, such as a small SCSI disk or a PATA flash module on the onboard IDE controller, and the root file system on SATA. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-February.mbox#L2136|"Re: Compatible IDE controllers (preferably for an ES45)"]], awinbow, debian-alpha, 25 Feb 2016)]
  * **AHCI** controllers are not enabled in Debian's Alpha kernel configuration, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2018-December.mbox#L1429|"Re: Use SMP kernel for Alpha (udeb) builds"]], Bob Tracy, debian-alpha, 8 Dec 2018)] and no reports of them working on Alpha are known.
  * **Radeon.** By Linux 3.11 the ''radeon'' module had become too large to load on Alpha, failing with "Relocation (type 4) overflow", and had to be built into the kernel. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2013-August.mbox#L808|"Re: new radeon firmware for 3.11.0-rcX"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 25 Aug 2013)] [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2014-December.mbox#L394|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] radeon questions"]], Don Wilburn, gentoo-alpha, 13 Dec 2014)] Linux 6.3 fixed the module loader, so that even the largest modules now load. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/b6b17a8b3ecd|"alpha: fix R_ALPHA_LITERAL reloc for large modules"]], Linux 6.3)] Linux 5.11 introduced a hard lockup with a Radeon HD 4350 in an XP1000, bisected to the radeon driver's new memory allocator. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2021-April.mbox#L137|"Re: X11 system lockup with 5.11.0 kernel"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 5 Apr 2021)] Current kernels also need current radeon firmware from ''linux-firmware''. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2013-August.mbox#L713|"new radeon firmware for 3.11.0-rcX"]], Bob Tracy, debian-alpha, 22 Aug 2013)]
  * **USB 3.** No reports of xHCI cards under Linux on Alpha are known. Under NetBSD, a USB 3 card works on the DS20L, even through a PCIe to PCI adapter. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/netbsd-port-alpha-mbox/gmane-2018-December.mbox#L1|"PCI on Alpha issues and DS20L usability"]], John Klos, port-alpha, 6 Dec 2018)]
  * **Intel PCIe cards** (''e1000e'') and newer LSI SAS cards (''mpt2sas'', ''mpt3sas'') need a PCIe slot, which no Alpha has; no reports of them behind a bridge are known.

=== TGA and TGA2 ===

The ZLXp-E cards use Digital's DC21030 "TGA" chip. The ZLXp-E1 is 8-plane, the E2 24-plane, and the E3 24-plane with a Z-buffer and 16MB. The Multia has the equivalent of an E1 built into the motherboard. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-October.mbox#L44573|"Re: TGA graphics stuff (feature question)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 27 Oct 1996)] The kernel's ''tgafb'' driver supports all three. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst?h=v6.10|Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst]], Linux 6.10)] The ZLXp-L cards use a different accelerator chipset and are not supported. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-November.mbox#L21028|"Re: ZLXp-L Graphics Cards"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Nov 1998)]

The TGA2 cards, the PowerStorm 3D30 and 4D20, use a Cirrus VGA chip for the text-mode console and the TGA2 chip for accelerated X. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-January.mbox#L18272|"Re: Graphics in my PWS433au Miata"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 23 Jan 2002)] XFree86 supported them from 4.0.2, at first limited to 1024x768 at 72 Hz. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-February.mbox#L2660|"Re: LinuxAlpha hardware support"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Feb 2001)] The kernel framebuffer driver does not support them, but they can be used with the standard VGA text console driver. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst?h=v6.10|Documentation/fb/tgafb.rst]], Linux 6.10)] The PBXGB-AA's switch should be set to position six (1024x768 at 72Hz). [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)] The PowerStorm 350 has no X driver. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L10236|"Re: 7.2 iso's and Powerstorm video card"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Dec 2002)]

=== ATI Mach64 ===

The ATI Mach64 was a lower-cost option for the AlphaStation 200 4/100; its jumper JU10, which enables the card's interrupt request, must be installed in that system. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphastation_200:alphastation_200_mach64_installation_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaStation 200 4/100 mach64 Graphics Accelerator Installation Guide}}, February 1995, pp. 1-3)]

=== PC graphics cards ===

Cards reported to work well include:

  * the ELSA GLoria Synergy, which passed both console and X tests on an AlphaServer 1200, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L5533|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 4 Apr 2002)]
  * Permedia 3 cards such as the Oxygen VX1, which have 32 MiB of memory and a fast RAMDAC, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-April.mbox#L1474|"Re: Looking for a "good" video card for a DS5305"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Apr 2002)]
  * the Matrox Millennium I and II, G200, and G400, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L4242|"fedora x install"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 28 Apr 2008)] which are good choices for 2D work, with 3D only on AGP cards, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-September.mbox#L7813|"Re: Cirrus Logic alternative chipset?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 7 Sep 2001)]
  * the 3dfx Voodoo3 3000, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-April.mbox#L4378|"Another test iso image from AC1.0"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Apr 2005)] whose 3D support stopped working on Alpha when X dropped the Glide library, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2007-April.mbox#L330|"Voodoo 3 pci"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 24 Apr 2007)]
  * ATI Radeons, which are the best choice for EV6 and later systems. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L4437|"fedora x install"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 28 Apr 2008)]

S3 Trio64 cards are unreliable, particularly in the AlphaStation 200 and 400, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-March.mbox#L17110|"Re: more pain in the arse"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Mar 2000)] and the Trio64V+ works poorly. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-July.mbox#L2797|"Re: XF86 for S3 Trio 64 164SX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Jul 2003)] On the UP1000 and UP1100, AGP cards work in PCI mode, but AGP GART support has never been made to work. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-June.mbox#L12190|"Re: XFree support of Alpha"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 17 Jun 2001)]

Current X.Org support on Alpha is limited. X.Org 1.13 left many legacy cards, such as the Permedia 2 and the S3 Trio64V2, with only basic 2D drivers, while a PCI Radeon HD 3450 with the ''radeon'' KMS driver ran a 1920x1200 display from a DS20 in 2021. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2021-October.mbox#L532|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] Alpha gfx card support for Xorg"]], Jason Howe, gentoo-alpha, 15 Oct 2021)] The ''radeon'' X driver needs the kernel's KMS driver, since the old user mode setting code was removed from it. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/gentoo-alpha-mbox/2014-December.mbox#L310|"Re: [gentoo-alpha] Re: radeon questions"]], Alex Deucher, gentoo-alpha, 11 Dec 2014)] By 2016, with the older X drivers removed, the ''radeon'' KMS driver was probably the only one still working on Alpha. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2016-April.mbox#L636|"Re: Multia state of support"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 3 Apr 2016)]

=== Console restrictions ===

  * On the AlphaServer 4000 and 4100, "VGAs configured as console display devices must be in PCI0." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-5)]
  * On the ES40, "Only one VGA graphic controller card, that is VGA-Enabled, is supported in PCI0 bus slots." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)]
  * With SRM V5.9 and earlier on the ES40, an Adaptec controller in a lower-numbered slot than an Oxygen VX1 "may cause a console hang". [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 19)]
  * On the UX164 (Ruffian), the VGA card must be in the single 64-bit slot, because it is in front of the built-in PCI-PCI bridge. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-June.mbox#L26949|"Re: Installing Linux on AlphaPC 164UX"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 12 Jun 1998)]
  * The AlphaServer 800's onboard S3 Trio64 must be disabled with jumper J27 to use another VGA card. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_800_users_guide.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 800 User's Guide}}, November 1997, p. 11-9)]

| KZPAC | PCI UltraSCSI RAID, 1 to 3 channels | Mylex DAC960PU | ''myrb'' (''DAC960'' in older kernels) |
| KZPEA | PCI dual-channel Ultra3 SCSI | Adaptec AIC-7899 | ''aic7xxx'' |
| KZPCC-CE | PCI three-channel Ultra2 RAID | DPT | ''dpt_i2o'' to Linux 5.19 |
| KGPSA | PCI Fibre Channel | Emulex LightPulse | ''lpfc'' |

Sources: the AlphaServer 4100 firmware release notes, [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-4)] the ES40 firmware release notes, [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, pp. 9, 38)] and the kernel's ''myrb'' driver, which names the KZPSC and KZPAC. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/drivers/scsi/myrb.c?h=v6.10|drivers/scsi/myrb.c]], Linux 6.10)] Peter Rival of Compaq identified the KZPBA-CA as a QLogic ISP1040B [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-October.mbox#L53498|"Re: ds10 install query"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 29 Oct 1999)] and the KGPSA-CA as an Emulex adapter, not a QLogic one. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-December.mbox#L13600|"Re: FC with fabric topology"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 12 Dec 2000)] The KZPSA is a Digital design, not a Mylex card, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-November.mbox#L3270|"Re: KZPSA driver (repost?)"]], Peter Rival, axp-list, 5 Nov 2001)] using a Symbios 53C720 and an Intel i960. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-November.mbox#L854|"KZPSA driver?"]], Barry Roberts, axp-list, 1 Nov 2001)] The ''dpt_i2o'' driver was removed in Linux 6.0. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/b04e75a4a8a8|"scsi: dpt_i2o: Remove obsolete driver"]], Linux 6.0)]

=== Booting from SRM ===

The AlphaServer 4000 and 4100 SRM console can boot from the KFPSA, KZPDA, KZPSA, KZPSC, KZPAC, KZPBA, KZPAA, and CIPCA storage adapters, the DE435, DE450, DE500, and DEFPA network adapters, the floppy drive, and the CD-ROM. It "does not support any EISA options as boot devices." [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-4)] The SRM console for evaluation boards and OEM motherboards has drivers for the NCR 810 and QLogic ISP1020 SCSI controllers, and the DC21040 and DE205 Ethernet controllers. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:common:srm_console_user_guide.pdf?linkonly|Alpha SRM Console for Alpha Microprocessor Motherboards User's Guide}}, September 1997, p. 1-3)]

For third-party cards, the SRM console supports QLogic ISP1020 and ISP1040 SCSI controllers and most NCR/Symbios controllers except the 885. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-July.mbox#L505|"Re: PWS433a and tru64"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 4 Jul 2000)] Originally only ARC and AlphaBIOS supported Adaptec controllers, and the consoles do not support BusLogic cards. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-June.mbox#L15694|"Re: Any1 with a working PC164!"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 13 Jun 1997)] Later SRM releases added some Adaptec controllers, the Intel EEPro100, the NCR/Symbios 896, and some Mylex RAID controllers. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2000-September.mbox#L1114|"Re: srm and scsi controllers."]], Jay Estabrook, debian-alpha, 6 Sep 2000)] SRM can boot from Adaptec cards on the AlphaPC 164LX and 164SX with firmware 5.8, and on the DS10, DS20, ES40, and XP1000 with 5.9. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2001-April.mbox#L35378|"Re: Alpha PC164, SRM, and Adaptec 2940UW"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 11 Apr 2001)] Some DVD drives are incompatible with the SRM console, usually because of their block size. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L12579|"Re: would like to do more upgrades"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Dec 2002)]

=== Mylex DAC960 ===

Digital sold Mylex DAC960 RAID controllers with its own firmware. The Linux driver required firmware 2.73 or later, but many of the older DAC960s sold by Digital have Digital's custom firmware 2.70 or older. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-March.mbox#L2019|"Re: digital server 7305, DAC960PU"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 4 Mar 2003)] The kernel accepts firmware 2.70 on Alpha. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/drivers/scsi/myrb.c?h=v6.10|drivers/scsi/myrb.c]], Linux 6.10)] SRM can boot from DAC960 volumes. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2003-February.mbox#L19351|"DAC960 Alpha support (was: Re: Redhat 7.2 on a DEC ALPHA 2100)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 26 Feb 2003)]

=== Other storage controllers ===

  * **QLogic:** the AlphaPC 164LX errata note "Compatibility problems ... between the AlphaPC 164LX motherboard and QLogic SCSI controller chips (ISP1020 and ISP1040)." [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164lx:pc164lx_technical_reference_manual_errata.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164LX Motherboard Errata Sheet}}, June 1997, p. 1)]
  * **Adaptec:** the 2940UW and 39160 worked in 2004 tests. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-October.mbox#L2929|"AC-0.9 Testing Results (was: Re: Alpha Core ISO Images)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 15 Oct 2004)] Jay Estabrook did the initial Alpha support in the ''aic7xxx'' driver. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/drivers/scsi/aic7xxx/aic7xxx_osm.c?h=v6.10|drivers/scsi/aic7xxx/aic7xxx_osm.c]], Linux 6.10)] The KZPEA must have its bus termination jumpers installed, or the console may hang. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)]
  * **IDE:** the AlphaPC 164 uses a CMD646 for onboard IDE. [(>{{wiki:hardware:motherboards:pc164:pc164_user_manual.pdf?linkonly|AlphaPC 164 Motherboard User's Manual}}, p. 3)] An add-in PCI controller, such as one using a CMD649 or Promise chip, is an alternative to weak onboard IDE. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2004-April.mbox#L2047|"Second IDE port not working on PC164"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 8 Apr 2004)]

The DE450's chip is the DC21041. [(>''EtherWORKS Turbo PCI 10 Adapter'', Digital infosheet, 9 October 1995, p. 2)] The DE600 is based on the Intel 82557, 82558, or 82559, and works with the EV6 consoles and some EV56 consoles, such as those of the LX164 and SX164. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-August.mbox#L31580|"Re: How to setup my 3 Com 3C509B ISA cards for Alpha machine?"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 2 Aug 2000)] The ''defxx'' driver supports the DEFEA and DEFPA. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/pub/scm/linux/kernel/git/torvalds/linux.git/tree/drivers/net/fddi/defxx.c?h=v6.10|drivers/net/fddi/defxx.c]], Linux 6.10)] The ''de4x5'' driver was removed in Linux 5.19. [(>[[https://git.kernel.org/torvalds/c/32c53420d2a0|"eth: de4x5: remove support for Generic DECchip & DIGITAL EtherWORKS PCI/EISA"]], Linux 5.19)]

At 100 Mbit/s, the 21142 and 21143 sometimes need boot arguments to force the ''tulip'' driver to use a specific media type. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-March.mbox#L57005|"Re: XP1000 and Linux"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 18 Mar 1999)] Digital recommended against DE500 auto-negotiation when the network speed is known, because it can delay booting considerably. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_4000_4100_v6.0_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer 4100/4000 Firmware Release Notes, V6.0}}, September 2001, p. 3-7)] The SRM console is particular about third-party Tulip cards. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1999-December.mbox#L47109|"Re: PC164, SRM and ethernet"]], Matthew Jacob, axp-list, 29 Dec 1999)]

Other cards reported to work include NE2000 clones, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L8828|"Re: Ethernet card question"]], David Mosberger, axp-list, 28 Mar 1996)] the RTL8139, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2002-May.mbox#L12933|"Re: Realtek 8139 metwork card"]], Falk Hueffner, debian-alpha, 18 May 2002)] the RTL8169, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-June.mbox#L30|"GigE on ES4x servers"]], Balint Cristian, axp-list, 1 Jun 2005)] and the DEGPA-SA with the ''acenic'' driver. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2005-June.mbox#L1|"GigE on ES4x servers"]], Gary Molenkamp, axp-list, 1 Jun 2005)]

[(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L23336|"Re: Sound on Miata (PWS500a)"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 9 Jul 1998)] Under ALSA it works with ''snd-sb8''.   * The M-Audio Revolution 7.1 works on the XP1000 and PWS 600au. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2008-April.mbox#L1|"Re: M-Audio Revolution 7.1 sound card causing crash on Alpha"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 1 Apr 2008)]

==== USB ====

Onboard USB is not recommended; an add-in card is the better option. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2002-December.mbox#L12579|"Re: would like to do more upgrades"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Dec 2002)] VIA USB controllers in particular have problems on Alpha, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2012-June.mbox#L1840|"Re: USB cards for PWS 433au"]], Michael Cree, debian-alpha, 24 Jun 2012)] and USB cards for Alpha should use an NEC controller. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/debian-alpha-mbox/2012-June.mbox#L2873|"Re: new video card (was Re: USB cards for PWS 433au)"]], Bob Tracy, debian-alpha, 28 Jun 2012)]

==== Serial and multiport cards ====

Drivers that access registers in bus memory need porting to Alpha. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1996-March.mbox#L2952|"RE: How to complete a Jensen system..."]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 10 Mar 1996)] The Cyclades kernel driver supported all Cyclom-Y and Cyclades-Z boards on Alpha from 1997. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1997-August.mbox#L5784|"Cyclades Linux driver under Alpha"]], Ivan Passos, axp-list, 8 Aug 1997)]

Digital's own serial options included the DigiBoard-based CXI01 multiport cards and the PBXDA PCI 4, 8, and 16-port asynchronous adapters. [(>''AlphaServer 2100A Supported Options List'', Digital, March 4, 1997)] [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:personal_workstation:personal_workstation_a_au_series_catalog.pdf?linkonly|Compaq DPW Enhanced a-Series & au-Series}}, Systems and Options Catalog, September 2000, p. 7)]

==== Keyboards and mice ====

Digital's PC-style keyboards were the LK411 and the LK471 (for Windows NT and UNIX), and the LK461 had the OpenVMS layout. [(>''AlphaServer 1000A 5/333, 5/4xx, 5/5xx Supported Options List'', Digital, April 14, 1997, "KB & Mouse")] The SRM console switches to the serial console if the keyboard is unplugged, [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/1998-July.mbox#L2188|"Re: installation on PC164LX, SRM 5.1-3, serial console"]], Jay Estabrook, axp-list, 1 Jul 1998)] and is more selective about keyboards than AlphaBIOS. [(>[[https://github.com/alphalinux-org/mailing-list-archives/blob/main/axp-list-mbox/2000-April.mbox#L6165|"Re: WAS: RE: SRM serial -> keyboard NOW: 5300 server won't boot"]], Ivan Kokshaysky, axp-list, 7 Apr 2000)] On the ES40, removing the mouse at the SRM console stops the keyboard from responding until the mouse is plugged back in. [(>{{wiki:software:firmware:alphaserver_es40_v7.3_firmware_release_notes.pdf?linkonly|AlphaServer ES40 V7.3 Firmware Release Notes}}, April 2007, p. 20)] The EV7 systems use USB keyboards and mice. [(>{{wiki:hardware:systems:alphaserver_es47:alphaserver_es47_tower_quickspecs.pdf?linkonly|HP AlphaServer ES47 Tower QuickSpecs}}, November 2003, pp. 15-16)]
