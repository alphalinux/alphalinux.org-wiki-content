===== Community =====

==== Mailing lists ====

The Alpha/Linux kernel mailing list is **linux-alpha@vger.kernel.org**, hosted at [[https://subspace.kernel.org/vger.kernel.org.html|vger.kernel.org]]. To subscribe, send an email to **linux-alpha+subscribe@vger.kernel.org**; the subject and body are ignored. Use **linux-alpha+subscribe-digest@vger.kernel.org** to receive a daily digest instead, and **linux-alpha+unsubscribe@vger.kernel.org** to unsubscribe. The list may also be read over NNTP at **nntp://nntp.lore.kernel.org**. Archives are located on [[https://lore.kernel.org/linux-alpha/|lore.kernel.org]], covering April 2002 onward, and [[https://marc.info/?l=linux-alpha|marc.info]], covering May 1998 onward.

Both [[https://archives.gentoo.org/gentoo-alpha/|Gentoo]] and [[https://lists.debian.org/debian-alpha/|Debian]] have distribution-related Alpha mailing lists.

The [[https://mail-index.netbsd.org/port-alpha/|NetBSD Alpha]] and [[http://marc.info/?l=openbsd-alpha|OpenBSD Alpha]] mailing lists, both still active, also carry Alpha hardware and porting information.

The lists that carried most of the port's history, among them Red Hat's **axp-list** and the lists hosted on alphalinux.org, are gone, as are the Alpha newsgroups. Their archives, and the distributions that once supported Alpha, are described in [[history:community|Community History]].

==== IRC ====

General Alpha discussion takes place in **#alpha** on [[https://libera.chat/|Libera.Chat]], and Gentoo's Alpha port uses **#gentoo-alpha** on the same network.

==== Forums ====

Gentoo has a [[https://forums.gentoo.org/viewforum.php?f=32|Gentoo on Alternative Architectures forum]].

==== Distributions ====

Gentoo, Debian Ports, and T2 SDE produce current Alpha releases. They, and the distributions that supported Alpha in the past, are described on the [[:distributions|Distributions]] page.
